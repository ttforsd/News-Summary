## Major regional Events

*   **Japan Hit by Major Earthquake:**
    *   A 6.8 magnitude earthquake struck Kyushu island, with 19 people missing at a mall and factory.
    *   Caused power cuts, damaged roads, a castle wall, and a footbridge.
    *   **Impact:** Significant humanitarian crisis and infrastructure damage; tsunami warning issued then lifted.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cgewgxlpj3po](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cgewgxlpj3po))
*   **Devastating Wildfires in France and Spain:**
    *   Wildfires in France's Gironde region and Spain's Madrid area have forced over 300,000 evacuations.
    *   New 40C heatwaves are expected to exacerbate the already "historic" blazes.
    *   **Impact:** Widespread destruction, economic blow to tourism, and critical strain on firefighting resources.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5yd8gly1ydo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5yd8gly1ydo)), ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c235m71mrreo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c235m71mrreo)), ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/28/wildfires-burn-nearly-300000-acres-in-france-and-spain-as-heat-wave-looms.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/28/wildfires-burn-nearly-300000-acres-in-france-and-spain-as-heat-wave-looms.html))
*   **Ecuador Grapples with Drug War:**
    *   Police face widespread violence, corruption, and well-funded gangs moving cocaine to Europe and the US.
    *   Last year's murder rate was among the highest globally, despite a government crackdown.
    *   **Impact:** Severe internal security crisis, destabilizing the country and fueling international drug trade.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cdx7n1r54nno](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cdx7n1r54nno))
*   **Kenya Investigates Mass Elephant Deaths:**
    *   An urgent inquiry is underway into the mysterious deaths of 14 elephants near Amboseli National Park.
    *   Experts are examining carcasses and tissue samples for disease or poisoning.
    *   **Impact:** Significant concern for wildlife conservation in the region.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3w0d7yeqlxo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3w0d7yeqlxo))
*   **Japan Town Debates First Mosque:**

    *   Japan faces labor shortages, increasing reliance on foreign workers, challenging its identity.
    *   **Impact:** Highlights growing social and political tensions over immigration in Japan.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c36d79nd5w8o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c36d79nd5w8o))
*   **India's Youth Force Government Action:**
