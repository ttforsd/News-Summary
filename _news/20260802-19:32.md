## International Relations & Diplomacy

*   **US-Iran De-escalation and Complexities**
    *   President Trump announced the cancellation of planned strikes against Iran, indicating "perimeters of a deal" had been agreed with Iran and regional states.
    *   The proposed deal aims to fully open the Strait of Hormuz and end Iran's nuclear threat.
    *   Iran's acting Defence Minister views US threats as credible but denies requesting a strike halt, calling Trump's demands a "wish list."
    *   Saudi Arabia's Crown Prince emphasized dialogue to reduce regional tensions.
    *   US embassies warned citizens in the Middle East to be prepared for escalation amidst growing tensions.
    *   **Context:** Iran's complex power structure, including the Revolutionary Guards, complicates ceasefire enforcement, as different factions may have competing priorities regarding strategic leverage vs. economic relief. Maritime battlefields, including the Strait of Hormuz and Red Sea, are seeing increased drone and missile attacks, impacting global trade.

## Major Regional Events

*   **Gaza Conflict Intensifies After Disarmament Deal**
    *   Israeli military aircraft carried out strikes on Gaza City, Khan Younis, and Deir el-Balah, killing at least 13 people, including children.
    *   This follows Hamas's conditional agreement to a U.S.-brokered disarmament plan, which Israel's Energy Minister expressed skepticism about.
    *   Hamas insists disarmament depends on Israel honoring ceasefire commitments and ceasing attacks.
    *   Over 1,100 Palestinians have been killed since a supposed ceasefire last October.
    *   (Sources: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/czjlvvkzj20o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/czjlvvkzj20o), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/trump-hamas-disarmament-conditions.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/trump-hamas-disarmament-conditions.html))
*   **Europe Grapples with Extreme Heat and Wildfires**
    *   Italy has placed nearly all major cities on a "red" heat alert, with temperatures in the mid-30s Celsius.
    *   Greece continues to battle severe wildfires, where two firefighting helicopters collided, killing two crew members.
    *   Washington State in the US also declared a statewide emergency due to extensive wildfires, forcing evacuations.
    *   **Impact:** Europe is adapting infrastructure, with Eurostar trains designed for 55°C and railway tracks painted white to prevent heat-related disruptions. Climate change is a significant factor, with Europe warming at twice the global average.
    *   (Sources: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c1417713ve6o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c1417713ve6o), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cr7k4gdrgljo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cr7k4gdrgljo), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/01/europe-extreme-heat-climate-change-55c.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/01/europe-extreme-heat-climate-change-55c.html), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/cpd7912xyq4o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/cpd7912xyq4o))
