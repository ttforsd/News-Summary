    *   Iran denies requesting the halt in strikes; talks aim for a Strait of Hormuz deal and denuclearization.

## Major Regional Events

*   **Renewed Israeli strikes in Gaza.**
    *   At least 13 people, including children, killed in Israeli military strikes on Gaza City, Khan Younis, and Deir el-Balah.
    *   Comes days after Hamas agreed to a US-led disarmament plan.
    *   Hamas states disarmament depends on Israel honoring commitments, including ending attacks and increasing aid.
    *   Source: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/czjlvvkzj20o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/czjlvvkzj20o), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/trump-hamas-disarmament-conditions.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/trump-hamas-disarmament-conditions.html))
*   **Ukraine launches deep strikes into Russia.**
    *   Ukraine attacked a major Russian oil refinery and Engels airfield in Saratov region, and other targets.
    *   President Zelenskyy called these "long-range sanctions" against Russia's war financing infrastructure.
    *   Russia reported 8 deaths and shot down 635 drones, also claiming strikes on Ukrainian Black Sea ports.
    *   Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c998gg5pe09o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c998gg5pe09o)
*   **Ukraine prepares for tough winter ahead.**
    *   Ukrainian officials anticipate a difficult winter due to expected Russian energy infrastructure assaults.
    *   Kyiv faces acute shortages of air defense systems and seeks Western support.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/ukraine-russia-war-putin-zelenskyy-winter.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/ukraine-russia-war-putin-zelenskyy-winter.html)
*   **Deadly protests in Pakistan-administered Kashmir.**
    *   Dozens killed in Rawalakot during clashes between security forces and the Joint Awami Action Committee (JAAC).
    *   Protests are over demands for affordable electricity and flour; authorities label JAAC "foreign-funded terrorists."
    *   Internet access is cut, and food shortages are reported in the city.
    *   Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5yvqk69enko](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5yvqk69enko)
*   **Europe grapples with extreme heat and wildfires.**
    *   Italy issued "red" alerts for high temperatures in most major cities, with temperatures in the mid-30s C.
    *   Two firefighting helicopters collided in Greece while battling wildfires, killing two crew.
    *   Wildfires also scorched over 250,000 acres in Washington state, leading to evacuations.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cr7k4gdrgljo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cr7k4gdrgljo), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c1417713ve6o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c1417713ve6o), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/cpd7912xyq4o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/cpd7912xyq4o))
*   **Spain's migrant crisis in Ceuta sparks EU divisions.**
    *   Thousands of migrants entered Spain's exclave of Ceuta from Morocco, leading to 72 deaths.
    *   The crisis exposed EU divisions, with Italy suspending its Schengen agreement with Spain.
    *   Social media posts falsely advertised an easy path to Europe, fueling the influx.
    *   Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c62vl925dqdo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c62vl925dqdo)
*   **Bomb detonated at Moscow restaurant.**
    *   Three people were killed and 21 injured after a homemade explosive device detonated.
    *   A woman carrying explosives was stopped by a security guard at an Italian restaurant near the city center.
    *   Authorities suspect terrorism, possibly linked to the war in Ukraine.
    *   Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c86n4ljxp63o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c86n4ljxp63o)
*   **Mountaineer Nirmal Purja's body recovered after Pakistan avalanche.**
    *   Renowned British-Nepali mountaineer Nirmal Purja was found on Broad Peak, Pakistan, following an avalanche.
    *   His death was announced by Elite Expeditions, confirming 10 fatalities in the incident.
    *   Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/clyljq79kdko](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/clyljq79kdko)

    *   Amnesty International reports a significant increase in executions of Ethiopians in Saudi Arabia.
    *   Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c80nkgdnl4no](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c80nkgdnl4no)

## Global Economic & Market Movers

*   **US and Japan intervene to bolster Yen.**
    *   Both nations jointly intervened to halt the yen's slide, which reached a 40-year low.
    *   This is the first coordinated action since 2011, motivated by concerns over US Treasury markets.
    *   Impact: Yen strengthened to 157.70 per dollar after trading at 163.73.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cglj1pr0wjwo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cglj1pr0wjwo), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/yen-intervention-us-japan-trump-bessent-katayama.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/yen-intervention-us-japan-trump-bessent-katayama.html))
*   **Oil prices drop as Middle East tensions ease.**
    *   Brent and WTI crude fell over 5% following President Trump's announcement of Iran talks.
    *   OPEC+ approved a September output increase of approximately 188,000 barrels per day.
    *   Impact: Markets show optimism for diplomatic de-escalation; shipping risks persist.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/oil-prices-today-wti-brent-hormuz-trump-iran.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/oil-prices-today-wti-brent-hormuz-trump-iran.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/cnbc-daily-open-trump-ceasefire-nuclear-deal-strait-of-hormuz-opec-oil-output-.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/cnbc-daily-open-trump-ceasefire-nuclear-deal-strait-of-hormuz-opec-oil-output-.html))
*   **Big Tech companies show mixed financial results amid AI investments.**
    *   Amazon, Microsoft, and Alphabet saw market caps surge by $1.5 trillion due to strong cloud growth.
    *   Meta and Apple experienced market value declines; Apple cited memory shortages and supply constraints impacting its outlook.
    *   Impact: AI investments are significantly affecting corporate balance sheets.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/tech-earnings-cash-memory-ai.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/tech-earnings-cash-memory-ai.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/apple-aapl-amazon-amzn-stock-today.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/apple-aapl-amazon-amzn-stock-today.html))
*   **Exxon and Chevron report surging profits.**
    *   Both oil majors saw Q2 profits increase significantly, driven by rising oil prices from the Iran war.

    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/exxon-xom-chevron-cvx-q2-earnings.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/exxon-xom-chevron-cvx-q2-earnings.html)

    *   AI spending is linking the fortunes of US tech giants and Korean memory chipmakers (Samsung, SK Hynix).

## Science, Tech, & Health (Major Developments)

*   **Australia combines ancient and modern wildfire tech.**
    *   Australia integrates satellite imagery, drones, and AI with Indigenous knowledge for bushfire management.
    *   "Cultural burning" practices, using controlled blazes, help reduce fuel loads and prevent larger fires.
    *   Impact: Europe, facing severe wildfires, could benefit from Australia's extensive firefighting experience.
    *   Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/czdmvr984yyo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/czdmvr984yyo)
*   **US lawmakers scrutinize Chinese AI model use by US companies.**
    *   Lawmakers requested information from DoorDash regarding its use of Chinese AI models (e.g., Kimi K2.6).
    *   Concerns about national security implications of relying on PRC-developed AI systems are growing.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/us-lawmakers-doordash-chinese-ai-models.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/us-lawmakers-doordash-chinese-ai-models.html)
*   **Trump's AI executive order deadline nears.**
    *   Federal agencies face an August 1st deadline to develop a framework for AI regulation.
    *   Tech leaders are actively lobbying in Washington amid an intensifying debate over open-weight AI models.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/trump-ai-executive-order-nears-key-deadline-regulation-debate-heats-up.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/trump-ai-executive-order-nears-key-deadline-regulation-debate-heats-up.html)
*   **Europe adapts infrastructure to extreme heat.**
