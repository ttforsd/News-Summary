## Global Economic & Market Movers

*   **US States Sue Trump Administration Over Tariffs**
    *   A coalition of 25 US states is suing the administration over new tariffs of 10-12.5% on goods from 60 trading partners.
    *   The tariffs target countries including the UK, China, and the EU, ostensibly over forced labor concerns.
    *   Impact: States argue tariffs are an illegal tax on families and businesses, distorting trade.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cy4kp8jd0ppo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cy4kp8jd0ppo), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/twenty-five-states-sue-trump-administration-over-latest-global-tariffs.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/twenty-five-states-sue-trump-administration-over-latest-global-tariffs.html))
*   **US Intervenes to Support Japanese Yen**
    *   The US joined Japan in a rare coordinated intervention to prop up the yen after it slid to 40-year lows.
    *   Analysts express doubt about sustained yen strength without structural policy shifts from the Bank of Japan.
    *   Impact: Aims to stabilize currency markets and prevent destabilization of US Treasury markets.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/what-the-market-is-saying-about-the-us-intervention-to-prop-up-the-yen.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/what-the-market-is-saying-about-the-us-intervention-to-prop-up-the-yen.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/04/cnbc-daily-open-fast-and-furious-intervention-to-prop-up-the-yen-will-it-work.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/04/cnbc-daily-open-fast-and-furry-intervention-to-prop-up-the-yen-will-it-work.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/japan-yen-intervention-us-treasurys-euros-.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/japan-yen-intervention-us-treasurys-euros-.html))
*   **Oil Majors Report Soaring Profits**
    *   BP's net profit more than doubled to $5.7 billion in Q2, beating expectations.
    *   Saudi Aramco's Q2 profit jumped 33% to $33.4 billion, exceeding forecasts.
    *   Impact: Fueled by higher oil and gas prices amidst Middle East conflict and disrupted shipping.
    *   US President Trump criticized major oil companies for "making too much money."
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/04/bp-2q-earnings-oil-iran-war.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/04/bp-2q-earnings-oil-iran-war.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/04/saudi-aramco-profits-jump-33-in-second-quarter-as-iran-war-squeezes-oil-supply.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/04/saudi-aramco-profits-jump-33-in-second-quarter-as-iran-war- squeezes-oil-supply.html))
*   **China's Domestic Tourism Sector Weakens**
    *   Domestic tourism is underperforming expectations, with Hilton's China revenue per available room falling 2.2% year-on-year in Q2.
    *   Industry RevPAR (revenue per available room) deteriorated by 6% in July, with room rates falling for tourist hotspots.
    *   Impact: A bright spot in China's sluggish consumer economy is dimming, reflecting broader demand softness.

## Science, Tech, & Health (Major Developments)

    *   More than 30 state water systems in Minnesota faced "co-ordinated cyber-attack."
    *   FBI reports attacks in seven states degraded water operations; Cisa is probing possible Iranian connection.
    *   Impact: Experts warn of national security threat and risk to public confidence in water infrastructure.
    *   Source: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c934dq95zpgo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c934dq95zpgo))
*   **AI Industry Faces Economic and Competitive Challenges**
    *   Big Tech's investments in private AI companies like Anthropic and OpenAI are distorting corporate earnings.
    *   Companies like Microsoft, Amazon, and Alphabet booked significant investment gains that inflate overall earnings growth.
    *   Pricing AI services remains tricky due to unpredictable "token" consumption and rapidly changing economics.
    *   Hugging Face CEO states China is winning the AI race and dominating open models, potentially surpassing US frontier labs soon.
    *   Impact: Challenges in accurate financial reporting, sustainable pricing models, and international AI competition.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/big-techs-anthropic-and-openai-stakes-distort-corporate-earnings.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/big-techs-anthropic-and-openai-stakes-distort-corporate-earnings.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/hugging-face-china-ai-race-open-models.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/hugging-face-china-ai-race-open-models.html), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c872r52x7jgo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c872r52x7jgo))
*   **US White House to Review AI Model Testing Framework**
    *   The White House will host leading AI companies to discuss a new voluntary framework for testing the cybersecurity capabilities of advanced AI models.
    *   Participating developers could provide government early access (up to 30 days) to "covered frontier models."
    *   Impact: Aims to evaluate models for software vulnerabilities and sophisticated cyberattacks without mandatory licensing.
    *   Source: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/white-house-ai-companies-voluntary-framework-meeting.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/white-house-ai-companies-voluntary-framework-meeting.html))
*   **US Cyclospora Outbreak Claims First Deaths in Michigan**
    *   Michigan health officials confirmed the first two deaths linked to the ongoing cyclospora outbreak, a parasitic infection.
    *   Nearly 7,000 lab-confirmed cases reported nationwide across 45 states; Michigan is at the epicenter with over 11,000 cases.
    *   Impact: Raises concerns about foodborne illness, particularly from contaminated produce like lettuce.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c2k7px317eeo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c2k7px317eeo), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/michigan-confirms-first-two-deaths-in-cyclospora-outbreak.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/michigan-confirms-first-two-deaths-in-cyclospora-outbreak.html))
*   **Child Vaccination Rates Decline in the UK**
    *   Childhood vaccination rates have fallen across Britain over the last 15 years, with polio and MMR jab uptake declining significantly.
    *   Experts attribute the decline to fewer face-to-face contacts with medical professionals, not primarily anti-vax sentiment.
    *   Impact: Raises concerns about public health and the resurgence of preventable diseases like measles.
    *   Source: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c0qvlw2dd0no](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c0qvlw2dd0no))
*   **Gaza Tech Workers Find "Digital Lifeline" Amidst Conflict**
    *   Thousands of young Gazans are pursuing remote roles in the digital economy, freelancing for international clients.
    *   New co-working spaces utilize generators and solar panels to provide internet access, despite extensive damage to Gaza's infrastructure.
    *   Impact: Offers a critical income source and an escape from dire conditions, overcoming tight border restrictions and high unemployment rates.
    *   Source: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c1m1xm8ykk2o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c1m1xm8ykk2o))
