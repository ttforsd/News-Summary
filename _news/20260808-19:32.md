## Major Regional Events

*   **Ukraine War Escalates with Drone Attacks:**
    *   Russian forces are systematically targeting Ukrainian medics and healthcare facilities with drones.
    *   WHO reports over 3,100 attacks on healthcare since February 2022, indicating a deliberate strategy.
    *   Civilians near the frontlines are increasingly cut off from essential health services.
    *   Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3r073eqvrjo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3r073eqvrjo)
    *   A child was among three killed in Russian missile attacks near Kyiv.
    *   Ukrainian President Zelensky called for more US-made Patriot interceptor missiles, citing reduced supply.
    *   Ukraine also hit Russian oil refineries and Black Sea vessels with drones.
    *   Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cz7dy8gq99eo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cz7dy8gq99eo)
*   **North American Wildfires Force Mass Evacuations:**
    *   Canada's Bald Range wildfire doubled in size, forcing 20,000 residents from Summerland and Peachland to evacuate.
    *   In Spokane, Washington, unprecedented wildfires led to nearly 65,000 evacuations and destroyed many homes.
    *   An alleged arsonist has been charged in connection with one of the Spokane fires.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cx25dkwk3e3o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cx25dkwk3e3o), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvglpvgljzdo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvglpvgljzdo))
*   **Typhoon Dolphin Impacts Japan, Heads for China:**
    *   Typhoon Dolphin caused power outages for 44,000 buildings and injured five people in Okinawa, Japan.
    *   Flights to/from Okinawa's main airport were cancelled.
    *   The typhoon is expected to make landfall on China's eastern coast, prompting port and school closures.
    *   Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvglp2zlvrlo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvglp2zlvrlo)
*   **Thailand Vows Stricter Gun Laws After School Shooting:**
    *   Thailand's Prime Minister pledged new gun control laws after a 14-year-old killed eight people (grandparents, teachers, student) and injured 22.

    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cdewx6rew04o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cdewx6rew04o), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c07rxz03034o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c07rxz03034o))

## Science, Tech, & Health (Major Developments)

    *   The new facilities are expected to produce DRAM and NAND memory, with cleanrooms opening between 2028 and 2029.

    *   AI agents demonstrated autonomous capabilities in identifying vulnerabilities and executing attacks.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/08/hugging-face-ai-hack-cybersecurity-black-hat.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/08/hugging-face-ai-hack-cybersecurity-black-hat.html)
