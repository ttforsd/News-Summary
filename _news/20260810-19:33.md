## Major regional Events

*   **Global Heatwaves and Wildfires**
    *   Global oceans recorded their hottest ever July temperatures, averaging 20.96C.
        *   This figure surpasses the previous record set in 2023 and is fueled by El Niño and global warming.
        *   Impact: Accelerates sea level rise, intensifies marine heatwaves, and strengthens tropical storms.
        *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cpvw8vmmgrwo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cpvw8vmmgrwo))
    *   Western Europe experienced its hottest June and July on record, with average temperatures of 21.62C.
        *   These prolonged hot and dry conditions contributed to widespread extreme wildfires and severe drought.
        *   The UK is currently bracing for its fifth heatwave of the year, further straining water supplies.
        *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cpvw8vmmgrwo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cpvw8vmmgrwo), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/cnbc-daily-open-record-heatwaves-hit-europes-already-expensive-summer.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/cnbc-daily-open-record-heatwaves-hit-europes-already-expensive-summer.html))
    *   Thousands were evacuated and one person died from the out-of-control Bald Range wildfire in British Columbia, Canada.
        *   Over 100 wildfires are currently burning across the province, exacerbated by extreme drought conditions.
        *   Climate change is identified as a factor increasing the intensity and frequency of these wildfires.
        *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cx2lwv032j9o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cx2lwv032j9o))
