## International Relations & Diplomacy

*   **Middle East Peace Process Stalls**
    *   Israeli PM Netanyahu formally rejected President Trump's 15-point Gaza peace roadmap.
    *   Plan aims for Hamas disarmament and deployment of an international stabilization force.
    *   The White House views Netanyahu's stance as election rhetoric, given upcoming Israeli elections.
    *   Israel has reportedly paused strikes on Gaza, a condition previously accepted by Hamas.

*   **Ukraine Strikes Deep into Russian Territory**
    *   A Ukrainian drone strike on Russia's Tatarstan republic killed 13 people, including one child, and injured 75.
    *   The attack hit the Nizhnekamsk oil refinery, over 1,100 km from the Ukrainian border.
    *   Kyiv is increasing long-range strikes on Russian facilities supporting its war effort.
    *   Sources: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvgjvgv926po](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvgjvgv926po)
*   **US-Iran Standoff Over Strait of Hormuz Deepens**
    *   President Trump claimed the US has "100%" control of the Strait of Hormuz, demanding Iran pay reparations.
    *   Iran insists the vital waterway will remain closed until US lifts its naval blockade and sanctions.
    *   Trump signaled reliance on economic pressure rather than new military strikes on Iran.
    *   Sources: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-control-reparation-talks-.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-control-reparation-talks-.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-oman-ships-blockade-shipping.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-oman-ships-blockade-shipping.html)

    *   Australia's Sunrise Energy Metals received a $400 million US Department of Defense investment.
    *   Aims to develop the world's first primary scandium mine, challenging China's critical minerals dominance.
    *   Scandium is strategically important for defense, aerospace, and AI data centers.

## Major regional Events

*   **Deadly Earthquake Hits Colombia**
    *   A magnitude 7.4 earthquake struck western Colombia, killing at least 132 people and injuring over 480.
    *   A state of emergency was declared; major cities like Pereira and Cali experienced extensive damage.
    *   The quake was felt as far as Venezuela, Ecuador, and Panama; international aid has been offered.
    *   Sources: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c20e360lx0vo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c20e360lx0vo), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/cz6473llqpwo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/cz6473llqpwo)
*   **Wildfires Rage in Canada and France**
    *   Canada's British Columbia faces large, uncontrolled wildfires, forcing over 12,000 evacuations and causing one fatality.
    *   France reported its worst wildfire damage since WWII, with 420 arrests for fire-starting incidents.
    *   Extreme drought and heatwaves are intensifying these climate-driven events across both regions.
    *   Sources: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cx2lwv032j9o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cx2lwv032j9o), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvgxjn92x9jo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvgxjn92x9jo)
*   **Finland and Sweden Reconnect by Rail**
    *   A new passenger train service launched, linking northern Finland and Sweden for the first time in nearly 40 years.
    *   The route from Oulu to Haparanda re-establishes an important regional connection.
    *   This enables part of what is considered Europe's longest continuous rail journey.
    *   Sources: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cx2lwpzz6qjo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cx2lwpzz6qjo)

## Science, Tech, & Health (Major Developments)

*   **Global Oceans Hit Record High Temperatures**
    *   World oceans recorded their hottest July temperatures (20.96C average), influenced by El Niño.
    *   Driven by human-caused global warming, this leads to sea level rise and marine heatwaves.
    *   Western Europe experienced its hottest June and July, with the UK facing a fifth heatwave and severe drought.
    *   Sources: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cpvw8vmmgrwo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cpvw8vmmgrwo)
*   **US Vaccine Policy Sparks Controversy**
    *   President Trump signed an executive order to limit recommended childhood vaccines and split MMR shots.
    *   The order promotes a discredited link between vaccines and autism.
    *   Public health experts strongly oppose the move, warning of risks to child health and potential disease re-emergence.
    *   Sources: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ce3q5vl581wo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ce3q5vl581wo), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/trump-vaccine-executive-order-autism.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/trump-vaccine-executive-order-autism.html)
*   **Meta Open-Sources Key AI Model**
    *   Meta announced it will open-source its powerful AI model, Muse Spark 1.2, and new on-device models.
    *   CEO Mark Zuckerberg positioned this as a challenge to closed AI systems and foreign open-source alternatives.
    *   He called for US policy changes to support American open-source AI development.
    *   Sources: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/meta-muse-glimmer-open-weight-ai.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/meta-muse-glimmer-open-weight-ai.html)
*   **OpenAI Boosts Cybersecurity Initiative**
    *   OpenAI expanded its "Daybreak" cybersecurity program with new access tiers and launched a new AI model, GPT-5.6-Cyber.
    *   The initiative aims to enhance defense against evolving AI agent threats, following recent security incidents.
    *   Sources: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/open-ai-daybreak-cybersecurity.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/open-ai-daybreak-cybersecurity.html)

    *   A growing number of satellites and rocket parts create an increasingly congested and hazardous orbital environment.
