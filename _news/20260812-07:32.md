## Major regional Events

*   **Colombia Earthquake Aftermath:**
    *   A magnitude 7.4 earthquake caused widespread devastation, with over 180 confirmed deaths.
        *   Casualties: Potentially over 240 dead, 2,600 injured, and 200 still missing.
        *   Impact: Cities of Cali and Pereira are worst hit; 1,100 homes destroyed, 8,000 damaged.
        *   Response: Rescuers continue searching for survivors in collapsed buildings; a state of emergency is declared.
        *   Aid: The UN, US, Spain, and several Latin American countries are offering assistance.
        *   Security: Cali imposed a curfew to aid rescue efforts and deter looting. (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c20dqd9qwq4o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c20dqd9qwq4o), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/czxerlglgw5o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/czxerlglgw5o))
*   **Zimbabwe Boat Disaster:**
    *   A boat carrying between 95 and 114 people sank on Lake Kariba.
        *   Impact: Multiple fatalities feared; rescue operations are in progress.
        *   Cause: The vessel was reportedly overloaded and may have capsized in bad weather. (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/czxe2vyxd7do](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/czxe2vyxd7do))
*   **Lebanon Abolishes Death Penalty:**
    *   Lebanon's parliament voted to abolish the death penalty.
        *   Significance: It would be the first Middle Eastern country to formally end capital punishment.
