## International Relations & Diplomacy

*   **US-Iran Tensions Escalate with Maritime Action.**
    *   US military helicopter disabled a Panama-flagged cargo ship in the Gulf of Oman.
    *   The vessel reportedly ignored warnings, attempting to breach the US blockade of Iranian ports.
    *   This comes amidst ongoing US-Iran conflict and recent Houthi attacks in the Red Sea, causing 6 fatalities on a cargo ship.
    *   The combined incidents highlight widening security risks in crucial shipping lanes, impacting global oil prices.

    *   A 7.4-magnitude earthquake in western Colombia killed at least 188 people.
    *   Hundreds remain missing in cities like Cali and Pereira, with extensive building collapses.
    *   A woman was rescued alive after 36 hours, providing a moment of hope amid ongoing search efforts.

    *   President Mnangagwa declared a state of disaster as rescue operations continue.

## Global Economic & Market Movers

*   **US Inflation Shows Slight Easing in July.**
    *   Consumer Price Index (CPI) rose 3.4% year-on-year, a marginal decrease from June's 3.5%.
    *   Monthly inflation was 0.1%, mainly due to housing costs, while food and energy prices cooled.
    *   This moderation may allow the Federal Reserve more time before considering another rate hike in September.
    *   (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c0qv2nn1gpeo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c0qv2nn1gpeo), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/cpi-inflation-report-july-2026.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/cpi-inflation-report-july-2026.html))
*   **Ukraine Attack Hits Russian Grain Exports.**
    *   A large Ukrainian drone and missile strike damaged two major grain export terminals in Russia's Novorossiysk port.
    *   The attack targeted Russia's last significant Black Sea naval base.
    *   Russia's agriculture ministry is redirecting cargo flows, potentially impacting global wheat supply and food prices.
    *   (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5y49xp2wrno](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5y49xp2wrno))
*   **Strait of Hormuz Traffic Nears Three-Month Low Amid Tensions.**
    *   Ship traffic through the Strait of Hormuz is significantly reduced, near a three-month low.
    *   This reflects continued US-Iran tensions and diplomatic deadlock over reopening the vital oil corridor.
    *   The IEA forecasts a larger drop in 2026 global oil demand due to the closure's impact.
    *   (Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/strait-hormuz-ship-traffic-iran-war-deal.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/strait-hormuz-ship-traffic-iran-war-deal.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/hormuz-closure-squeezes-global-economy-as-oil-demand-destruction-intensifies-iea-says.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/hormuz-closure-squeezes-global-economy-as-oil-demand-destruction-intensifies-iea-says.html))
*   **Yen Continues Slide Despite US-Japan Intervention.**
    *   The Japanese yen trades near 159 per dollar, failing to hold gains from recent intervention.
    *   Persistent yield gap with US and carry trades maintain pressure on the currency.
    *   Experts suggest intervention only slows speculation; fundamental economic shifts are needed.
    *   (Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/why-the-historic-us-japan-intervention-has-failed-to-halt-the-yens-slide.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/why-the-historic-us-japan-intervention-has-failed-to-halt-the-yens-slide.html))
*   **India's Inflation Accelerates, Rate Hike Expected.**
    *   India's consumer price inflation rose to 4.45% in July, up from 4.38%.
    *   Driven by increased food and transport costs, firming expectations for an RBI rate hike later this year.
    *   (Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/india-inflation-july-rbi-rate-oil-prices.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/india-inflation-july-rbi-rate-oil-prices.html))
*   **Europe's Heatwave Strains Nuclear Power and Economy.**
    *   Extreme heat and low river levels threaten nuclear power generation across Europe.
    *   Romania may shut its last working reactor; France faces reduced output and jellyfish-related closures.
    *   Heat-related disruptions are projected to cost the EU €180 billion (1% of GDP) in 2026.
    *   (Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/scorching-heat-is-straining-europes-nuclear-power-supplies--and-prompting-drastic-measures.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/scorching-heat-is-straining-europes-nuclear-power-supplies--and-prompting-drastic-measures.html))
*   **Norway's Sovereign Wealth Fund Posts Record Profit.**
    *   The world's largest sovereign wealth fund (Norway's $2.3 trillion) reported a record $184 billion profit in H1.
    *   Gains were significantly driven by Asian technology stocks.
    *   Fund also revealed a new $1.2 billion stake in SpaceX.

    *   Electric vehicles (EVs) accounted for 65.1% of new passenger car sales in China in July.
