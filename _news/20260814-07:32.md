## International Relations & Diplomacy

*   **US-Iran Tensions & Strait of Hormuz**:
    *   The US Treasury announced "unprecedented" economic measures and an "indefinite" naval blockade against Iran.
    *   Iran disputes US control of the Strait of Hormuz, declaring it "remains blocked."
    *   Ship traffic through Hormuz is near a three-month low, down 90% since February.
    *   Iran demands an end to the US blockade, sanctions relief, troop withdrawals, and reparations.
    *   An Iranian advisor claims the US military is "weaker than we perceived."

*   **US Aircraft Carrier Conditions**:
    *   The USS Abraham Lincoln, deployed for over 250 days, faces reports of poor conditions, including food shortages and mental health issues.
    *   Lawmakers are demanding answers from the Pentagon amid claims of sailors attempting to jump overboard.
    *   The USS George Washington is reportedly en route to replace the USS Abraham Lincoln.
    *   (Sources: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cyvl2d5j52lo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cyvl2d5j52lo), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/14/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-carrier-abraham-lincoln-george-washington.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/14/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-carrier-abraham-lincoln-george-washington.html))

## Major regional Events

*   **Extreme Weather in Japan**:
    *   "Unprecedented" record rainfall in Chiba, Japan, killed four people and wounded five.
    *   Over 20,000 households lost power; a level five emergency warning was issued.
    *   Unusually active typhoon season affecting Japan and neighboring countries.
    *   (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq561gpen8lo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq561gpen8lo))
*   **European Heatwave & Energy Impact**:
    *   Romania shut down its Cernavodă nuclear plant due to critically low Danube river levels.
    *   Two reactors, supplying 20% of Romania's electricity, are expected to be offline for 10 days.
    *   Prolonged heatwave across Europe has caused record low river levels, impacting hydropower.
    *   (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqlxpq5q799o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqlxpq5q799o))
*   **Israeli-Palestinian Displacement**:
    *   Israeli military ordered a dozen Palestinian households in Qusra village, West Bank, to leave.
    *   Follows a settler siege cutting off power and water to three homes.
    *   The US Ambassador condemned settler actions as "horrific act of terror."
    *   (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cj4kppdk2qwo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cj4kppdk2qwo))
*   **Ukraine Attacks Russian Grain Ports**:
    *   Ukraine launched drone and missile attacks on Russian grain export terminals in Novorossiysk.
