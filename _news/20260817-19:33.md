## International Relations & Diplomacy

*   **US-Iran Ceasefire Expires Amid Heightened Tensions**
    *   The 60-day ceasefire between the US and Iran has expired without a new deal.
    *   President Trump threatened to "bomb" Oman if it "gets in the way" of US talks with Iran, citing Omani negotiations with Tehran over the Strait of Hormuz.
    *   Iran's Foreign Ministry denied direct US negotiations and ruled out extending the interim deal, threatening escalation.
    *   Impact: Oil prices surged (Brent to $91), and shipping through the Strait of Hormuz, vital for global oil, significantly slowed.

## Global Economic & Market Movers
