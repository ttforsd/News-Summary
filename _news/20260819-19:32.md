## Science, Tech, & Health (Major Developments)

*   **AI's Impact on Global Labor Markets**
    *   Goldman Sachs research indicates AI is slowing job growth in sectors like call centers, software publishing, and consulting across developed economies.
    *   Entry-level workers are particularly vulnerable to AI-driven automation.
    *   [Source: CNBC]
*   **US Efforts to Control Chinese AI Chip Access**
    *   The US is moving to close a loophole enabling Chinese AI firms to remotely access advanced Nvidia chips via data centers in Southeast Asia.
    *   Proposed legislation, the Remote Access Security Act (RASA), aims to regulate cloud-based access to controlled technology.
    *   [Source: CNBC]
*   **Nvidia's AI Data Center Expansion Strategy**
    *   Nvidia is acting as a "matchmaker" to connect companies needing GPUs with data center operators in the Nordics.
    *   This strategy expands Nvidia's influence beyond chip sales, capitalizing on the Nordics' abundant power and suitable climate for AI infrastructure.
    *   [Source: CNBC]
*   **Breakthrough Melanoma Vaccine**

    *   The mRNA-based shot, combined with Keytruda, significantly extended disease-free survival and reduced cancer spread.
    *   [Source: CNBC]
*   **Chinese Humanoid Robot Market Boom**
