## Global Economic & Market Movers

*   **US Debt Surpasses $40 Trillion; Treasury Intervenes**
    *   The US gross national debt exceeded $40 trillion, doubling in just over a decade.
    *   To stabilize bond markets and address surging yields, the Treasury Department announced it would double its government debt buybacks for longer-dated bonds.
    *   Political Context: President Trump criticized the Federal Reserve's high interest rates, claiming they unfairly burden debt financing.
    *   Sources: (CNBC: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/19/us-government-debt-passes-40-trillion-mark-for-the-first-time.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/19/us-government-debt-passes-40-trillion-mark-for-the-first-time.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/19/treasury-announces-upscaled-buyback-operation-for-longer-term-debt-sending-yields-lower.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/19/treasury-announces-upscaled-buyback-operation-for-longer-term-debt-sending-yields-lower.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/19/trump-bemoans-fed-interest-rate-policy-says-us-should-be-paying-much-less.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/19/trump-bemoans-fed-interest-rate-policy-says-us-should-be-paying-much-less.html))
*   **Evergrande Founder Sentenced to Life in China**
    *   Hui Ka Yan, founder of the collapsed property giant Evergrande, was sentenced to life in prison, with all personal property confiscated.
    *   His companies received a ¥15.82 billion fine for falsifying records and concealing debt.
    *   Impact: This marks a critical moment in China's ongoing property sector crisis and its economic fallout.
    *   Source: (BBC: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cd0x9mjjmgjo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cd0x9mjjmgjo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss))
