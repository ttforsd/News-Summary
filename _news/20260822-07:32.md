## Global Economic & Market Movers

*   **US Treasury Intervenes in Bond Market as Debt Swells:**
    *   The US Treasury announced plans to at least double bond buybacks for long-dated government debt to stabilize the market.
    *   JPMorgan warned this only shifts the debt problem, comparing it to "paying your mortgage with your credit card."
    *   US government debt topped $40 trillion for the first time, though Treasury Secretary Bessent believes the budget deficit may have peaked.
    *   Impact: Raises concerns about long-term US financial health and potential for higher risk premia in markets.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/21/us-bond-intervention-shifting-problem-future-jpmorgan.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/21/us-bond-intervention-shifting-problem-future-jpmorgan.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/21/cnbc-daily-open-the-bessent-bid-wares-off.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/21/cnbc-daily-open-the-bessent-bid-wares-off.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/20/bessent-budget-deficit-trump.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/20/bessent-budget-deficit-trump.html))

*   **Global Food Supply Faces "Perfect Storm":**
    *   Russian and Ukrainian attacks on Black Sea infrastructure, high fertilizer prices, and extreme weather threaten global food supply.
    *   Grain prices are soaring, and analysts fear farmers may not plant usual levels of winter crops.
    *   Impact: Risks significant food security issues and continued inflationary pressures on food commodities globally.
    *   Source: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/21/grain-price-russia-food-supply.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/21/grain-price-russia-food-supply.html))

    *   Impact: Highlights the growing domestic economic impact of the war in Ukraine on Russia, forcing it to import fuel and allow lower-grade petrol.
