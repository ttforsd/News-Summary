## International Relations & Diplomacy

*   **US Launches "Economic D-Day" Against Iran; Tehran Vows Resistance.**
    *   The US Treasury Secretary announced a major economic offensive aimed at isolating Iran from the global financial system.
    *   Nations and entities engaging financially with Iran face severe US penalties and isolation.
    *   Iran's economy minister declared the country "fully prepared" with a two-year plan to counter sanctions, anticipating resistance from China and Russia.
    *   Iran threatened to halt oil exports from the region and impose transit rules in the Strait of Hormuz.
    *   Oman's Foreign Minister is scheduled to visit Tehran for discussions regarding the Strait of Hormuz.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c0qxew81y83o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c0qxew81y83o)), ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/24/trump-iran-economy-sanctions-china-bessent.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/24/trump-iran-economy-sanctions-china-bessent.html)), ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/24/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-bessent-economic-sanctions-.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/24/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-bessent-economic-sanctions-.html)), ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/us-iran-secondary-sanctions-china-india-uae-hormuz-trade-.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/us-iran-secondary-sanctions-china-india-uae-hormuz-trade-.html))

*   **US Removes Syria from Terrorism Sponsor List.**
    *   The US has removed Syria from its list of state sponsors of terrorism after 47 years.
    *   This action aims to remove barriers to private sector investment and facilitate Syria's global economic reintegration.
    *   The US cited Syria's positive steps to disengage from international terrorism.
    *   Syria's new President Ahmed al-Sharaa and his group, Hayat Tahrir al-Sham (HTS), were also delisted from US terrorist designations.
    *   Source: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c33knmyl0y5o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c33knmyl0y5o))

*   **US-Canada Trade War Escalates.**
    *   President Donald Trump announced a planned 50% tariff increase on Canadian automobiles, trucks, and auto parts, effective January 1, 2027.
    *   The escalation follows a breakdown in recent trade negotiations, with both nations attributing the failure to "unreasonable" demands from the other.
    *   Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney pledged "dollar for dollar" retaliatory tariffs on various US goods starting September 8.
    *   The trade dispute raises concerns about potential negative impacts on businesses and the integrity of the USMCA trade pact.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c93vpd60654o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c93vpd60654o)), ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/24/trump-canada-auto-tariffs-trade-war.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/24/trump-canada-auto-tariffs-trade-war.html)), ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c05rj80ve56o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c05rj80ve56o)), ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cy0j0zz8l83o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cy0j0zz8l83o)), ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/22/us-canada-trade-talks-collapse-ushering-in-wave-of-new-tariffs.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/22/us-canada-trade-talks-collapse-ushering-in-wave-of-new-tariffs.html))

*   **Lockerbie Bombing Trial Postponed.**
    *   The trial of Abu Agila Mohammad Kheir Al-Marimi, accused in the 1988 Lockerbie bombing, has been postponed a third time.
    *   The delay stems from newly discovered evidence, pushing the trial start to January 2027.
    *   Source: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq63rmj1zl1o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq63rmj1zl1o))

## Major regional Events

*   **European Allies Boost Ukraine's Missile Capabilities; Russia Warns of Retaliation.**
    *   The UK is providing Ukraine with blueprints for Scalp/Storm Shadow long-range cruise missile components for domestic production.
    *   France pledged additional interceptor missiles to strengthen Ukraine's air defenses.
    *   UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham met President Zelenskyy in Kyiv to reconfirm support and chair a "Coalition of the Willing" meeting.
    *   A Kremlin adviser warned of potential "semi-military" attacks by "unknown sources" on UK drone factories in response to Western aid.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cg598m3meeyo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cg598m3meeyo)), ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/cx2znjly4dno](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/cx2znjly4dno)), ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/24/france-uk-step-up-missile-support-for-ukraine-as-european-leaders-meet.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/24/france-uk-step-up-missile-support-for-ukraine-as-european-leaders-meet.html))

*   **Ukrainian Drone Strikes Target Russian Online Retailers.**
    *   Ukrainian drone attacks hit Ozon, Russia's second-largest online retailer, in multiple southern regions.
    *   These strikes follow weeks of similar targeting of Wildberries, Russia's largest online retailer, which Ukraine claims supplies military components.
    *   Source: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cg598yvrzpro](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cg598yvrzpro))

*   **Israel Warns Hamas Over Gaza Border Incidents.**
    *   Israel's Defence Minister issued a "forceful" warning to Hamas regarding the launching of kites, drones, and balloons from Gaza.
    *   Israel declared a "zero tolerance" policy for such launches, irrespective of whether they carry explosives.
    *   Hamas disavowed responsibility for the recent non-explosive kites, describing them as "children's toys" and accusing Israel of using them as a pretext for continued aggression.
    *   Source: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5y58g2dp3xo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5y58g2dp3xo))

*   **Taliban Rule in Afghanistan Five Years On.**
    *   A BBC investigation highlighted the dire realities under Taliban rule five years after their takeover.
    *   Women face severe restrictions on education, employment, and personal freedoms, contributing to widespread despair.
    *   Taliban leaders justify their policies through their interpretation of Sharia law, while the country grapples with pervasive poverty and economic instability.
    *   Source: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckgvk9p2n7ko](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckgvk9p2n7ko))

*   **Zambian Courts Closed Ahead of Election Petition Deadline.**
    *   Court premises in Lusaka and other parts of Zambia were sealed off by police as the deadline to challenge recent presidential election results approached.
    *   Opposition candidate Brian Mundubile plans to contest the outcome, alleging irregularities in the vote.
    *   The Law Association of Zambia raised concerns about restrictions on access to justice and potential implications for judicial independence.
    *   Source: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cgrvjn88rxqo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cgrvjn88rxqo))

*   **Tornado Causes Damage in Southern France.**
    *   A destructive tornado swept through villages in southern France, injuring over 40 people and damaging homes.
    *   The severe weather, with winds exceeding 100 km/h, led to power outages for thousands of households.
    *   Source: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/ckg8wkd0j7do](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/ckg8wkd0j7do))

*   **Elephant Poisoning Investigation in Kenya.**
