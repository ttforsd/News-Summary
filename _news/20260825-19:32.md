## International Relations & Diplomacy

*   **US-Canada Trade War Escalates with Retaliatory Tariffs:**
    *   Canada announced C$28bn ($20bn) in retaliatory tariffs (up to 50%) on various US goods starting September 8th.
    *   Impact: Response to earlier US tariffs; President Trump threatened 50% tariffs on Canadian automobiles from January 1st.
    *   Context: Trade talks between the two nations collapsed last week; supply chains and consumers face increased costs.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3v4xg5klx7o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3v4xg5klx7o), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/canada-trump-tariffs-trade-carney-leblanc.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/canada-trump-tariffs-trade-carney-leblanc.html), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c05rj80ve56o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c05rj80ve56o), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/24/trump-canada-auto-tariffs-trade-war.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/24/trump-canada-auto-tariffs-trade-war.html))
*   **US Imposes New Sanctions on Iran and Trading Partners:**
    *   The US launched "Operation Economic Outcast" to isolate Iran economically, threatening penalties against entities doing business with Tehran.
    *   Response: China criticized the "illegal unilateral sanctions" and vowed to protect its interests.
    *   Impact: Oil prices fell, suggesting reduced fears of renewed military conflict in the Middle East.
    *   Context: Follows the expiration of a 60-day ceasefire in the Iran war; Iran and Oman are discussing joint shipping routes and mine clearing in the Strait of Hormuz.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/clyl8965j5go](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/clyl8965j5go), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/us-iran-secondary-sanctions-china-india-uae-hormuz-trade-.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/us-iran-secondary-sanctions-china-india-uae-hormuz-trade-.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/china-iran-us-sanctions-banks-cips.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/china-iran-us-sanctions-banks-cips.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/oil-prices-iran-trump-hormuz.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/oil-prices-iran-trump-hormuz.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/iran-oman-strait-hormuz-oil.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/iran-oman-strait-hormuz-oil.html))

## Science, Tech, & Health (Major Developments)

*   **High Prevalence of Brain Disease in NFL Players:**
    *   A new study in the British Medical Journal indicates that up to 97.7% of NFL players who died between 2016-2021 showed signs of Chronic Traumatic Encephalopathy (CTE).
    *   Impact: Researchers urge preventative measures against repetitive head impacts at all levels of American football.
    *   Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/american-football/articles/cew9l95kyp0o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/american-football/articles/cew9l95kyp0o)
*   **Measles Cases Soar in the US, Linked to Vaccine Hesitancy:**
    *   Two unvaccinated individuals died of measles in Pennsylvania amid the highest US infection rates in 35 years.
    *   Context: Vaccination rates in affected areas are below the 95% needed for herd immunity; President Trump's executive order on vaccines faces criticism.
    *   Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5yednen30go](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5yednen30go)
