## Global Economic & Market Movers

*   **US Inflation Rate Shows Slight Rise**
    *   The Federal Reserve's preferred inflation gauge, the Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) price index, rose 0.2% in July, with annual inflation at 3.7%.
    *   Core PCE, excluding food and energy, increased 0.2% monthly and 3.3% annually, aligning with forecasts.
    *   Context: Data released ahead of the Federal Open Market Committee meeting in September and the Jackson Hole symposium.
    *   Source: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/feds-preferred-inflation-gauge-shows-core-prices-rose-3point3percent-annually-in-july.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/feds-preferred-inflation-gauge-shows-core-prices-rose-3point3percent-annually-in-july.html))

    *   Context: Follows long-term borrowing costs hitting a near two-decade high and the US Treasury's bond market intervention.
    *   Impact: Warsh's remarks could significantly influence expectations for Fed policy and trigger market activity in bonds and the dollar.
    *   Source: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/dollar-and-bond-markets-on-edge-ahead-of-jackson-hole-as-bessents-market-intervention-piles-pressure-on-warsh.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/dollar-and-bond-markets-on-edge-ahead-of-jackson-hole-as-bessents-market-intervention-piles-pressure-on-warsh.html))

    *   Requirements: Includes daily usage limits, "nighttime blocks" for teenagers, enhanced age assurance, and parental tools.
    *   Impact: Significant regulatory changes for Meta and potential precedent for other social media companies.
