## 主要區域事件

*   **尼泊爾-西藏邊境洪災危機**
    *   中國警告尼泊爾-西藏邊境新形成的堰塞湖有「高風險」決堤，可能引發第二次洪災。
    *   洪災由山體冰川崩塌引發，已造成尼泊爾至少469人死亡、數百人失蹤，西藏亦有558人失蹤。
    *   中國當局已暫停當地搜救行動；尼泊爾呼籲民眾遠離河流。
    *   包括英國在內的國際社會已承諾提供援助，協助尼泊爾應對災害。
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckg8l2x8wr8o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckg8l2x8wr8o)), ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cd948kn02e5o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cd948kn02e5o)), ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cd08z840p8lo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cd08z840p8lo)), ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/cp80m87pez3o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/cp80m87pez3o)), ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/ckgxnrg8j19o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/ckgxnrg8j19o))
