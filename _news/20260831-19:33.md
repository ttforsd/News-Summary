## Major Regional Events

*   **Nepal Floods: Rescue Efforts Underway for Hydropower Workers.**
    *   Over 600 workers are believed trapped in mud-filled tunnels along the Trishuli River after flash floods.
    *   Death toll from Nepal-Tibet border disaster reaches 939, with 3,925 missing.
    *   International teams using explosives and air pumps to reach survivors; 279 rescued from sites.
    *   Impact: Significant humanitarian crisis; severe damage to at least 11 hydropower projects critical for Nepal's energy.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5ylvgq9q71o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5ylvgq9q71o)), ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/c4g79v842xjo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/c4g79v842xjo)), ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/c5ydwy004v0o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/c5ydwy004v0o))

*   **Grand Canyon Hit by Deadly Flash Floods.**
    *   Two confirmed dead and 14 missing/unaccounted for after flash floods in Bright Angel Canyon.
    *   Flooding swept away footbridges and caused extensive damage to park infrastructure, including the Transcanyon Waterline.
    *   Monsoon season rains and heavy downpours are forecast to continue, posing ongoing risks.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/clylkjyez1do](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/clylkjyez1do))

*   **Ferry Capsizes Off Northern Cyprus, Children Missing.**
    *   A ferry traveling from Kyrenia to Turkey capsized, killing 8 people with 20 still missing, including children.
    *   Rough seas and a dual-wave impact cited as factors for the vessel overturning.
    *   Eight crew members, including the captain, were arrested; three-day national mourning declared.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c1mv5ggg1l8o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c1mv5ggg1l8o))

*   **Algeria Seeks Death Penalty for Arsonists After Forest Fires.**
