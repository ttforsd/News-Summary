## International Relations & Diplomacy

*   **US-Iran Tensions Escalate**
    *   Iran launched missile and drone attacks at US bases in Jordan, Kuwait, Iraq, and Bahrain.
    *   This was in retaliation for US strikes on Iranian targets, which the US linked to attacks on Strait of Hormuz shipping.
    *   The Iranian Red Crescent reported 5 civilian deaths, including a child, from a US missile in Sirik; the US denied targeting civilians.
    *   US President Trump asserted US control over the Strait of Hormuz, stating negotiations are not being forced.
    *   This marks a renewed escalation after a month-long pause in military exchanges and the expiry of a ceasefire agreement in June.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cj06q4ynpmjo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cj06q4ynpmjo), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-irgc-jordan-bahrain.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-irgc-jordan-bahrain.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/iran-markets-doom-loop.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/iran-markets-doom-loop.html))
*   **Germany Accuses Russia of Drone Attack**
    *   Germany identified Russia as responsible for a drone attack at Leipzig/Halle Airport targeting Ukrainian cargo planes.
    *   Interior Minister Dobrindt cited a "well-established pattern of Russian hybrid operations."
    *   Germany announced closure of the Russian consulate in Bonn and termination of the Russian House contract.
    *   Russia denied involvement, calling German measures an "unprecedented escalation" and promising reciprocal responses.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5ylm3m67n2o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5ylm3m67n2o))
*   **Ukraine Warns Airlines on Russian Airspace**
    *   President Zelensky urged international airlines to avoid Russian airspace due to intensified Ukrainian drone strikes.
    *   He stated the "Russian sky is becoming completely dangerous" for civilian aviation.
    *   Russian President Putin denounced Zelensky's comments as "declaration of state terrorism."
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c9v9yydy2wvo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c9v9yydy2wvo))
*   **US-Canada Trade Spat Intensifies**
    *   US President Trump ordered Lake Ontario renamed "Lake America" on Apple Maps for US users.
    *   Canadian Prime Minister Carney criticized the US administration's actions as "doing memes" and "throwing shade."
    *   Canada pledged to implement "dollar for dollar" retaliatory tariffs following new US levies.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cddvyyz0e47o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cddvyyz0e47o), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/canada-carney-trump-trade-ontario.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/canada-carney-trump-trade-ontario.html))
*   **China's Cautious Diplomacy with Iran**
    *   Chinese President Xi Jinping met briefly and discreetly with Iranian President Pezeshkian at the SCO summit.
    *   This move aims to balance relations and avoid appearing too close to Tehran ahead of Xi's planned summit with President Trump.
    *   SCO leaders jointly condemned military strikes on Iran and supported member states' rights to civilian nuclear development.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/china-trump-iran-summit-nuclear-trade-tariffs-sanctions-.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/china-trump-iran-summit-nuclear-trade-tariffs-sanctions-.html))
*   **India Rejects Water Treaty Ruling with Pakistan**
    *   India rejected a Permanent Court of Arbitration (PCA) ruling that it must uphold the 1960 Indus Waters Treaty with Pakistan.
    *   The PCA ruled in Pakistan's favor, but India disputes the court's jurisdiction over its sovereign decisions.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/india-hague-ruling-pakistan-water-treaty.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/india-hague-ruling-pakistan-water-treaty.html))

## Major Regional Events

*   **Israel Expands Presence in Southern Lebanon**
    *   Israel is increasing its military footprint in southern Lebanon, now occupying about 5% of Lebanese territory.
    *   UNIFIL reported new Israeli bases and defensive infrastructure in the occupied zone.
    *   Concerns rise over destruction of civilian infrastructure, displacing hundreds of thousands of Lebanese.
    *   Lebanese officials allege over 5,000 Israeli violations since the June ceasefire.
    *   Source: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cge41d19gq9o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cge41d19gq9o))
*   **Nepal Hit by Deadly Flash Floods**
    *   Devastating flash floods in Nepal have resulted in over 1,000 deaths and left approximately 4,000 people missing.
    *   Hydropower workers were trapped in tunnels; rescue operations are ongoing.
    *   Experts criticized the lack of effective early warning systems despite prior signs of glacier instability.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cg49590v6k1o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cg49590v6k1o))
*   **Mass Deportations to Afghanistan**
    *   Six million Afghans have been forcibly returned to Afghanistan from Pakistan and Iran in less than three years.
    *   The UN Refugee Agency (UNHCR) labeled this the largest cross-border movement of people globally.
    *   Many deportees, including those born in Pakistan, face an uncertain future with limited resources in Taliban-controlled Afghanistan.
    *   Source: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c2e074nn8eko](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c2e074nn8eko))
*   **Hong Kong Activist Pleads Guilty**
    *   Prominent pro-democracy activist Joshua Wong pleaded guilty to "collusion with foreign entities" under the National Security Law.
    *   He faces a maximum sentence of life in prison, underscoring Beijing's stringent enforcement of the law.
    *   Source: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cx2zmzg0408o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cx2zmzg0408o))
*   **Norway's New Monarch**
    *   Haakon VIII has officially taken the oath as the new King of Norway, following the recent death of his father, King Harald V.
    *   Source: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/ceq8945e71ro](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/ceq8945e71ro))

## Global Economic & Market Movers

*   **Oil Market Surge Amid Mideast Tensions**
    *   Global oil prices, including Brent and West Texas Intermediate (WTI), surged over 4% following renewed US-Iran hostilities.
    *   The US Treasury Secretary noted potential future bypasses for the Strait of Hormuz via land pipelines within two years.
    *   CME Group is introducing smaller 10-barrel WTI crude futures contracts, increasing market accessibility for retail investors.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-irgc-jordan-bahrain.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-irgc-jordan-bahrain.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/iran-markets-doom-loop.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/iran-markets-doom-loop.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/the-3-trillion-oil-market-is-becoming-more-accessible-than-ever-no-longer-a-rich-mans-game.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/the-3-trillion-oil-market-is-becoming-more-accessible-than-ever-no-longer-a-rich-mans-game.html))
*   **Global Bond Yields Hit Multi-Decade Highs**
    *   Government bond yields in the US, Japan, UK, and Germany rose significantly, with some reaching levels not seen since the 1990s or early 2000s.
    *   The surge is attributed to heightened Middle East turmoil exacerbating inflation concerns and a hawkish outlook from central banks.
    *   Fed Governor Michael Barr signaled readiness to support another rate hike if inflation persists.
    *   Eurozone inflation climbed to 3.3% in August, highest since September 2024, raising expectations for an ECB rate hike.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/bond-yields-iran-inflation-treasurys-japan-uk.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/bond-yields-iran-inflation-treasurys-japan-uk.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/10-year-yield-hits-highest-since-january-2025-as-higher-oil-prices-stoke-inflation-worries.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/10-year-yield-hits-highest-since-january-2025-as-higher-oil-prices-stoke-inflation-worries.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/fed-governor-barr-says-hell-support-rate-hike-if-inflation-doesnt-ease.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/fed-governor-barr-says-hell-support-rate-hike-if-inflation-doesnt-ease.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/euro-zone-inflation-rate-hike.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/euro-zone-inflation-rate-hike.html))
*   **China's Stance on Trade at G20**
    *   China was the sole nation to dissent from a G20 statement criticizing "non-market based economies pushing out a never-ending stream of cheap exports."
    *   US Treasury Secretary Bessent highlighted the need to resolve these trade imbalances, impacting global trade discussions.
    *   Source: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/bessent-china-g20-trade-exports-trump-xi.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/bessent-china-g20-trade-exports-trump-xi.html))
*   **Emerging Markets Attract Carry Trades**
    *   Emerging economies like Brazil, Turkey, and Colombia are increasingly favored for carry trades.
    *   This trend is bolstered by a weaker US dollar following US Treasury bond buybacks, offering higher yields.
    *   Source: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/these-emerging-markets-are-favored-to-get-a-wall-of-money-from-carry-trades.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/these-emerging-markets-are-favored-to-get-a-wall-of-money-from-carry-trades.html))
*   **Asian Financial Stability Reviewed**
    *   HSBC's chief economist observed notable parallels between the current financial climate and the period before the 1997 Asian Financial Crisis, including high US yields and tech optimism.
    *   However, he noted that Asia's shift to a capital exporter position reduces financial vulnerability but creates "demand vulnerability" tied to the US AI hardware boom.
    *   Source: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/a-rerun-of-the-1997-asian-financial-crisis-hsbcs-chief-economist-sees-significant-similarities.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/a-rerun-of-the-1997-asian-financial-crisis-hsbcs-chief-economist-sees-significant-similarities.html))
*   **Australia's Economy Outperforms**
    *   Australia recorded a 2.1% year-on-year economic growth in the second quarter, exceeding market expectations.
    *   Growth was primarily driven by private demand and mining exports, despite subdued household spending.
    *   The robust GDP figures provide the Reserve Bank of Australia flexibility for further monetary policy tightening to control inflation.
