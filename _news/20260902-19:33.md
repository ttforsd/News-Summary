## International Relations & Diplomacy

*   **US-Iran Military Exchanges Escalate Amid Civilian Accusations**
    *   Iran accused the US of a "war crime" after a missile strike reportedly killed four people, including two children, at a wedding in southern Iran.
    *   The US Central Command (Centcom) denied targeting civilians, stating its strikes on Iran targeted air defense sites, radar systems, and maritime assets.
    *   Iran retaliated by launching missiles and drones at US targets in Jordan, Kuwait, Iraq, and Bahrain; most were reportedly intercepted.
    *   Saudi Arabia condemned an Iranian attack on its oil tanker *Sidr* in the Strait of Hormuz, which resulted in the deaths of two Filipino sailors.
    *   Iran’s Revolutionary Guard claimed two tankers hit naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz, a claim disputed by Centcom as disinformation.
    *   US Energy Secretary Chris Wright reported a record 17 million barrels of oil transited Hormuz on Monday, aided by US military protection.
    *   President Trump floated renaming the Strait of Hormuz the "Trump Strait" and emphasized economic pressure over negotiation with Iran.
    *   China maintained a public distance from Iran at the SCO summit, signaling a careful diplomatic approach ahead of a planned summit with Trump.

## Major regional Events

*   **Nepal Floods Prompt Massive Relief Efforts and Economic Warnings**
    *   Catastrophic flash floods along the Nepal-Tibet border have killed over 1,200 and left more than 4,200 missing, with 21 deaths and 540 unaccounted for on the Tibetan side.
    *   Relief and recovery operations are intensifying amid challenging conditions, with over 40 bridges destroyed limiting access to remote areas.
    *   Nepal's mountain tourism industry faces a "serious warning" from cancellations, with estimated rebuilding costs of $4-$5 billion.
    *   The UN and Nepal's PM highlight the escalating risks from climate change in the Himalayan region and the need for international support.
    *   Source: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8xk45k9r1go](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8xk45k9r1go)), ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/nepal-tibet-floods-adventure-tourism-economy.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/nepal-tibet-floods-adventure-tourism-economy.html))

*   **Continuing Casualties in Gaza Despite Ceasefire**
    *   More than 900 people have been killed in Israeli attacks in Gaza this year, despite a ceasefire declared on October 10, according to the Hamas-run Ministry of Health.
    *   The total Palestinian death toll since the ceasefire is over 1,300, including 301 children.
    *   BBC Verify analysis and UN figures suggest consistent Israeli strikes, with July being the deadliest month this year.
    *   The Israel Defense Forces (IDF) disputes the figures, stating its operations are precise and comply with the agreement, targeting military operatives.
    *   Source: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c7708dy33pdo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c7708dy33pdo))

    *   Lebanese officials report over 5,000 Israeli violations of the June ceasefire deal.

    *   Israel states actions target Hezbollah for military purposes and are in accordance with international law.

## Global Economic & Market Movers

*   **Global Bond Rout Accelerates on Inflation Fears**
    *   Benchmark government bond yields worldwide continued to climb, hitting multi-decade highs.
    *   Japan's 10-year yield exceeded 3%, a 30-year high; German bunds reached 3.378% (highest since 2011); British gilts hit a post-2008 high of 5.25%.
    *   The sell-off is attributed to resurging inflation, expected interest rate hikes by central banks, and concerns over high government debt loads.
    *   Equity markets also entered a "risk-off" mode, with major indices falling for three consecutive sessions.
    *   Source: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/global-bond-rout-gathers-pace-as-inflation-fears-mount.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/global-bond-rout-gathers-pace-as-inflation-fears-mount.html))

*   **US Finalizes Significant Venezuela Oil Deal**
    *   The US signed a deal granting a US-led company 100-year concessions over 17 Venezuelan oilfields, totaling 65 billion barrels of crude.
    *   Chevron announced a $7 billion investment to more than double its Venezuelan oil production to 600,000 barrels per day by 2031.
    *   The deal is seen by the Trump administration as "shifting the geopolitical center of the global energy markets" away from the Middle East.
    *   Critics, including former US officials and Venezuelan opposition, labeled it a "terrible deal" and "new colonialism" due to one-sided terms and lack of democratic progress.
    *   Source: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c93vkgk5e1yo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c93vkgk5e1yo)), ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/chevron-venezuela-operations.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/chevron-venezuela-operations.html))

*   **Canadian Central Bank Holds Rates Amid US Tariff Dispute**
    *   The Bank of Canada held its key interest rate at 2.25%, balancing accelerating GDP growth with rising inflation and the impact of new US tariffs.
    *   The US imposed 50% tariffs on various Canadian goods, with Canada planning retaliation, creating economic uncertainty.
    *   Source: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/bank-canada-rate-decision-trump-tariffs.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/bank-canada-rate-decision-trump-tariffs.html))

*   **Australia's Economy Outperforms, Inflation Concerns Remain**
    *   Australia posted a 2.1% GDP growth in the second quarter, exceeding expectations, driven by private demand and mining exports.
