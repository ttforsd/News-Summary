## International Relations & Diplomacy

*   **UK Sanctions Israeli Settlements; Israel Retaliates**
    *   The UK announced sanctions on illegal Israeli West Bank settlements, banning imports and targeting entities supporting expansion.
    *   Foreign Secretary Miliband labeled the occupation unlawful and cited "ethnic cleansing" by settlers.
    *   Israel's Foreign Minister Saar denounced the UK's move as an "outrageous lie," closing the UK's East Jerusalem consulate.
    *   Israel also banned 11 UK MPs from entering the country.
    *   France, Canada, and ten other European nations pledged similar national or EU-level trade restrictions.
    *   The US stated it would not follow the UK's action, with former US Ambassador to Israel warning of economic repercussions for British businesses.
    *   The UK additionally imposed sanctions on Hezbollah's financing wing and Iran.

*   **US-Canada Trade Tensions Deepen**
    *   Canada implemented retaliatory tariffs of up to 50% on C$28 billion ($20 billion) worth of US goods.
    *   Prime Minister Mark Carney affirmed the necessity of trade diversification from the US, acknowledging potential costs.
    *   US President Trump threatened to block Canadian aerospace firm Bombardier from the US market unless it builds products domestically.
