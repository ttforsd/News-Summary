## International Relations & Diplomacy

*   **Middle East Security and Energy Routes**
    *   Saudi Arabia's East-West oil pipeline was shut down after drone attacks launched from Iraq, impacting global oil flows.
        *   Iraq acknowledged the origin and is investigating, with an operations commander removed from his post in Maysan governorate.
        *   The pipeline offers a crucial bypass to the Strait of Hormuz amidst regional conflict.
        *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c62m933465eo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c62m933465eo), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/11/saudi-arabia-shut-down-east-west-crude-oil-pipeline.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/11/saudi-arabia-shut-down-east-west-crude-oil-pipeline.html))
