## Executive summary

*   **Geopolitical Tensions Rise:** A Russian warship fired flares at a Danish helicopter in the Baltic Sea, increasing regional friction, while the US confirmed deploying offensive space weapons, raising arms race concerns.
*   **Middle East Instability:** An oil tanker was attacked in the Strait of Hormuz, and a critical Saudi oil pipeline was damaged, leading to soaring oil prices and highlighting the escalating US-Iran conflict.
*   **AI Regulation Divide:** Tech leaders advocate for slowing AI development due to existential risks, but US President Trump dismisses concerns as a "hoax," creating political deadlock on regulation in Congress.
*   **Global Economic Headwinds:** US Treasury yields hit 19-year highs as markets anticipate multiple Fed rate hikes, while China faces slowing retail sales, deepening investment slumps, and tight capital outflow controls.
