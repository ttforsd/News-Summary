## International Relations & Diplomacy
*   **US and Denmark formalize Greenland security deal:**
    *   Grants US "permanent control over security" in Greenland, addressing strategic Arctic concerns.
    *   Includes provisions to bar non-NATO military presence or "sensitive investments" by adversaries (Russia/China).
    *   Deal is perpetual, affirming US access and ability to build military structures.
    *   Will be signed at the UN General Assembly next week.
    *   Sources: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c63d7lexyym1o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c63d7lexyym1o), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/trump-greenland-denmark-military-deal.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/trump-greenland-denmark-military-deal.html)
*   **Trump advances $24.3 billion F-35 jet sale to Saudi Arabia:**

    *   Aims to enhance Saudi defense capabilities against Houthi attacks.
    *   Lawmakers worry about technology transfer to China and regional military balance.
    *   Sources: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3x2zrn01pxko](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3x2zrn01pxko), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/trump-saudi-arabia-f-35-houthis-iran-war.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/trump-saudi-arabia-f-35-houthis-iran-war.html)
