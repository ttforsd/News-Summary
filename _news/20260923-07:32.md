## Executive summary

*   US and Iran held their first mediated talks since June amidst escalating rhetoric, with Iran setting conditions to reopen the Strait of Hormuz.
*   The US finalized a defense deal with Denmark and Greenland, enhancing its military presence in the Arctic.
*   The EU's decision to lift sanctions on two Russian oligarchs sparked anger in Ukraine, while Russia welcomed the move.
*   Global AI development continues at a rapid pace, with US-China talks focusing on AI, new models released, and significant industry investments.
*   The El Niño climate phenomenon has reached its strongest recorded level, signaling potential widespread global weather disruptions.

## International Relations & Diplomacy

*   **US and Iran Hold Talks Amidst Tensions:**
    *   US and Iranian officials met at the UN General Assembly, mediated by Qatar, the first talks since June.
    *   US President Trump issued a threat to "annihilate" Iran but also suggested a deal could come after midterm elections.
    *   Iran presented conditions for de-escalation, including ending US "acts of aggression" and lifting the naval blockade.
    *   The G7 nations condemned Iran's arming and support of Houthi rebels in Yemen, calling for an end to their attacks.
    *   Impact: Oil prices saw fluctuations, reflecting diplomatic hopes and ongoing regional instability concerns.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ck62m1631d7po](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ck62m1631d7po), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/23/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/23/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz.html), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwm2qpmepmlzo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwm2qpmepmlzo), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/22/trump-un-speech-today.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/22/trump-un-speech-today.html))

*   **US Strengthens Arctic Presence with Greenland Deal:**
    *   The US, Denmark, and Greenland signed a defense agreement at the UN, allowing the US to build two new military bases in Greenland.
    *   The deal reaffirms Denmark's sovereignty over Greenland and prohibits non-NATO countries from establishing military installations or persistent military presence.
    *   Impact: Enhances US and NATO security in the strategically important Arctic region.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c9dr74g32g8go](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c9dr74g32g8go), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/22/trump-greenland-security-deal-military-bases-.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/22/trump-greenland-security-deal-military-bases-.html))

*   **EU Removes Russian Oligarchs from Sanctions List:**
    *   The European Union decided to lift sanctions on two prominent Russian oligarchs, Alisher Usmanov and Mikhail Fridman.
    *   France advocated for Usmanov's removal citing national security, while Luxembourg supported Fridman's removal due to a legal challenge.
    *   Ukraine condemned the move, stating it sends the "wrong signal" to Moscow; Latvia plans national sanctions.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6dj4k98107do](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6dj4k98107do))

*   **UK and US Leaders Meet at UN General Assembly:**
    *   UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham met US President Donald Trump for the first time, discussing closer cooperation.
    *   Topics included stability in Ukraine, resolving Middle East conflicts, and international AI standards.
    *   A new UK-US AI and Autonomy partnership was announced to protect critical national infrastructure.
    *   The Chagos Islands deal, involving a joint US-UK military base, remains paused due to Trump's withdrawn support.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqevwpmvpekeo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqevwpmvpekeo), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq5y4zpn655ko](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq5y4zpn655ko), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cxly57mv4767o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cxly57mv4767o))

*   **Lithuania Prepares for Russian Threats:**
    *   Lithuania's Prime Minister affirmed the country's readiness to "fight back" against Russian aggression, despite having civilian evacuation plans.
    *   The country faces "hybrid attacks" blamed on close Russian ally Belarus, including drone incursions into its airspace.
    *   Impact: Highlights ongoing security concerns on NATO's eastern flank and the deployment of NATO troops.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ck5yw8npxzrlo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ck5yw8npxzrlo))

*   **US-China Summit Looms with Key Agendas:**
    *   US President Trump and Chinese leader Xi Jinping will meet in Washington, focusing on trade stability and economic deals.
    *   Discussions will navigate complex issues including artificial intelligence, ongoing tariffs, and Iran war-related sanctions.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/21/trump-xi-china-summit-trade-tariffs.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/21/trump-xi-china-sum-”it-trade-tariffs.html))

*   **Israel-Palestine Tensions Impact International Football:**
    *   The Republic of Ireland's national football team will play "away" matches against Israel in neutral stadiums.
    *   The decision follows significant opposition from fans and politicians regarding Israel's military actions in Gaza.
    *   Ireland's government has maintained a strong pro-Palestinian stance, including officially recognizing a Palestinian state.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/ckz7z5w7ey2jo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/ckz7z5w7ey2jo))

*   **US White House Press Access Disputed:**
    *   The US Department of Justice defended President Trump's ban of three news outlets from the White House, asserting access is a "privilege, not a right."
    *   This follows a lawsuit by the banned outlets (CNN, Politico, MS NOW) alleging viewpoint discrimination.
    *   Impact: Raises significant concerns about press freedom and government transparency in the US.

## Major regional Events

    *   Naufer Moulavi, identified as the mastermind, was among those convicted.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqy7z275yx65o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqy7z275yx65o))
