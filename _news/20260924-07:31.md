## Global Economic & Market Movers

*   **Global Bond Yields Soar to Multi-Decade Highs:**
    *   US 10-year Treasury yield hit 5.104%, highest since July 2007; Japan's 10-year JGB yield rose to a 30-year high of 3.055%.
    *   **Drivers:** Stronger-than-expected US economic activity, hawkish Federal Reserve comments, weak Treasury auction demand, and high oil prices contributed to the spike.
    *   **Impact:** Higher borrowing costs are anticipated for consumers and businesses, potentially slowing economic activity.
    *   **Sources:** ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/23/treasury-yields-oil-inflation-fed.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/23/treasury-yields-oil-inflation-fed.html)), ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/24/historic-day-for-global-bonds-as-10-year-treasury-and-jgb-yields-hit-highest-in-decades.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/24/historic-day-for-global-bonds-as-10-year-treasury-and-jgb-yields-hit-highest-in-decades.html)), ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/23/what-happens-to-the-economy-when-treasury-yields-soar.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/23/what-happens-to-the-economy-when-treasury-yields-soar.html))
