## Executive Summary

*   **Ukraine Frontline Stabilizes; Europe Warned to Prepare:** Ukraine's former top general claims Russia's offensive capability is broken due to heavy losses; urges European allies to prepare for potential war with Russia.
*   **Black Sea Escalation:** Bulgaria summoned Russia's ambassador after two merchant ships were hit by drones in its economic zone, with one sinking. NATO is monitoring the escalating security environment.
*   **IMF Cautions on Global Economy:** The IMF slightly raised its 2026 global growth forecast due to AI investment, but warned of increasing public debt, persistent inflation, and "disorderly" spikes in bond yields.
*   **AI Market Volatility & Concerns:** Global chip stocks plummeted due to intensified Chinese competition and broader worries about AI financing. AI lab Anthropic warns its models are nearing self-replication across home devices, raising significant safety risks.
*   **Cyberattacks Target Critical Infrastructure:** French supermarkets suffered widespread fridge malfunctions and fires due to a cyberattack, while Ukraine alleges Russian strikes on its energy infrastructure despite a supposed "energy ceasefire."
*   **Falklands Oil Dispute Escalates:** Argentina initiated legal action against the UK at an international tribunal over oil exploration near the Falkland Islands.
*   **Extreme El Niño Deepens:** The current El Niño event continues to strengthen, setting new central Pacific sea-surface temperature records and threatening more extreme weather globally.
