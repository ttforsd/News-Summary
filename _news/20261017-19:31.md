## Geopolitical & Security Dynamics

*   **US Waives Russia Sanctions Amid Ukraine Tensions:** President Trump issued an executive order waiving new sanctions and tariffs on Russia, citing U.S. national interest.
    *   Detail: The sanctions, passed by Congress, targeted Russia's economy and top oil/gas purchasers (e.g., China, India).
    *   Context: This follows an earlier temporary easing of sanctions for Russian diesel imports to address rising U.S. fuel prices.
    *   Impact: Ukrainian President Zelenskyy and Italian Defence Minister Guido Crosetto condemned the moves, fearing it emboldens Russia and undermines Western unity.

    *   Source: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/16/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/16/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz.html))

*   **Strait of Hormuz Shipping Risks:** Commercial vessels continue to navigate the Strait of Hormuz despite recent attacks on Singapore-flagged ships.

*   **Norway Considers Investing in Defense Companies:** Norway's $2.3 trillion sovereign wealth fund may change ethics rules to invest in more Western defense firms.
