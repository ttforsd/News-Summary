## Major Regional Events

*   **Deadly High-Speed Train Crash in Spain**
    *   At least 68 people died and dozens were injured in a collision between two high-speed trains near Madrid.
    *   Cause: A computer system failure followed by an "inexplicable" human error by the control center, allowing a second train to proceed without speed restrictions.
    *   Response: Spain declared three days of mourning; Prime Minister Sánchez vowed a thorough investigation into potential negligence.
    *   Context: This is Spain's second fatal high-speed rail crash this year.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6p3g84kjqjlo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6p3g84kjqjlo)), ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm8xz7pkd0jjo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm8xz7pkd0jjo)), ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/spain-high-speed-train-crash.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/spain-high-speed-train-crash.html))

*   **Israel's General Election Results in Deadlock**
    *   Partial results show incumbent Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's bloc leading with 53 seats, but falling short of a 61-seat majority in the 120-seat Knesset.
    *   Opposition: Rival Gadi Eisenkot's bloc secured 48 seats; Arab-led parties gained 19 seats, becoming potential kingmakers.
    *   Outlook: A new government formation will likely require significant compromises, or the country could face another election.
    *   Challenges: Deep divisions over religious-secular issues, military service, and post-7 October accountability persist.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmdx3knq1xexo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmdx3knq1xexo)), ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm62y46d97wwo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm62y46d97wwo)), ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqz0ve94ygpjo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqz0ve94ygpjo))

*   **Ex-Wife of Zimbabwe VP Freed After Case Collapse**

## Global Economic & Market Movers

*   **Federal Reserve Holds Interest Rates Steady**
    *   The U.S. Federal Reserve maintained its benchmark interest rate between 3.75%-4%.
    *   Reasoning: Committee emphasized a patient approach, awaiting more information on inflation's timely return to its 2% goal.
    *   Dissent: Two FOMC members, Lisa Cook and Beth Hammack, dissented, advocating for a quarter-percentage-point hike.
    *   Future Outlook: The Fed plans procedural changes for 2027, reducing scheduled rate adjustment opportunities.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/fed-interest-rate-decision-october-2026.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/fed-interest-rate-decision-october-2026.html)), ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/fed-rate-decision-warsh-takeaways.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/fed-rate-decision-warsh-takeaways.html))
