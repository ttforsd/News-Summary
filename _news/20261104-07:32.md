## International Relations & Diplomacy

*   **US Midterm Election Results Deliver Democratic Sweep**
    *   Democrats gained control of both the House of Representatives and the Senate, exceeding expectations.
    *   This outcome is seen as a significant rebuke to President Trump's policies and is expected to lead to increased congressional oversight.
    *   Impact: Will likely constrain Trump's legislative agenda for his remaining term and enable more investigations into his administration. *   **Escalation of US-Iran Tensions in Strait of Hormuz**
    *   Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) claims four oil tankers were destroyed by naval mines and two seized in the Strait of Hormuz.
    *   This follows reported US military strikes on Iran's Qeshm Island, described as a "defensive measure" against Iranian missile use.
    *   Impact: Heightens regional instability in a critical global shipping lane, risks global oil supply, and Iranian officials warn the US against further aggression. *   **UN Peacekeeping Mission Ends in Western Sahara**
    *   The UN Mission for the Referendum in Western Sahara (Minurso) mandate expired due to a deadlock in the UN Security Council between the US and Russia.
    *   Morocco, which claims the territory, welcomed the decision, seeing it as an opportunity to advance its autonomy proposal.
    *   Impact: Raises fears of renewed conflict in the region, as the decades-long territorial dispute remains unresolved and international monitoring ceases. (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqp8genleg2ro](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqp8genleg2ro))
*   **US Contractor Pleads Guilty to Sharing Ethiopia Intelligence**
    *   An Ethiopian-born American citizen, a former US contractor, pleaded guilty to passing highly classified intelligence to Ethiopia during its 2020 civil war.
    *   The information, including satellite imagery and military activities, was intended to help target Tigray fighters.
    *   Impact: Highlights vulnerabilities in national security to insider threats and foreign espionage. (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6y8lz73vmv7o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6y8lz73vmv7o))
