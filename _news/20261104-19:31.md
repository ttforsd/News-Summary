## Major regional Events

*   **U.S. Midterm Elections Deliver Democratic Sweep**
    *   Democrats secured control of both the House and Senate, a significant setback for President Donald Trump.
    *   Republicans largely blamed Trump's influence, unpopular policies, and focus on his personal legacy for the losses.
    *   Historic wins included Texas electing its first Latina Governor and first Democratic Senator in decades, and Michigan electing the first Muslim U.S. Senator.
    *   Congressional oversight on the administration is expected to intensify, with calls for investigations into various issues.

    *   Reduced deficit spending is expected, which could improve the fiscal outlook.

## Science, Tech, & Health (Major Developments)

*   **Cyberattacks Target European Cold Storage Infrastructure**
    *   French blood storage facilities, alongside supermarkets across several European countries, were impacted by cyberattacks on refrigeration systems.
    *   Hackers remotely raised temperatures in cold rooms, though French blood supplies were protected by rapid staff intervention.
    *   Ukraine and Russia also reported false emergency alerts due to hacks, indicating a broader landscape of cyber warfare.
    *   The culprits remain unconfirmed, with Russian or Iranian hacktivists and pranksters considered as possibilities.
    *   (Sources: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8vgxyvz6y9ro](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8vgxyvz6y9ro), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/cmwyvrgk9p0ro](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/cmwyvrgk9p0ro), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/crq5n6204g20o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/crq5n6204g20o), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/04/blood-bank-fridges-cyberattacks.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/04/blood-bank-fridges-cyberattacks.html))
