## Executive summary

*   **US Midterm Elections:** Democrats gained control of both Congress chambers in a "blue wave," setting the stage for increased oversight of the Trump administration and potential policy shifts on the Iran war, affordability, and tech regulation.
*   **Iran War & Oil Prices:** US political figures linked the unpopular Iran war to Republican midterm losses, leading to a significant drop in crude oil prices amid hopes for de-escalation. Iran's chief negotiator welcomed the election outcome as a "no to war" message.

## Major regional Events

*   **Hong Kong Pro-Democracy Activist Jailed for 10 Years**
    *   Joshua Wong, a prominent figure in Hong Kong's democracy movement, received a 10-year prison sentence.
    *   Charge: He was found guilty of colluding with foreign forces under the city's sweeping national security law.
    *   Context: Critics argue the law is used to silence dissent and erode Hong Kong's autonomy.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwp9g3rlp8jlo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwp9g3rlp8jlo))
*   **Hungary Releases Communist-Era Secret Police Files**
    *   Hungary has opened files on approximately 60,000 informers from the communist era, making them publicly available online.
    *   Purpose: The government states the move aims to help society heal from 45 years of communist rule.
    *   Controversy: Critics express concerns that the timing is late, files are incomplete, and may cause unnecessary anguish.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c977p01kgmmko](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c977p01kgmmko))
*   **Ukraine Reports Deadly Russian "Double-Tap" Bombing**
    *   Ukrainian police released bodycam video allegedly showing a Russian "double-tap" bomb strike in Kamyshuvakha village.
    *   Casualties: The attack reportedly killed at least 40 people, including civilians seeking aid and responding police officers.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c98rz6penv86o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c98rz6penv86o))
*   **East Asia Battered by Intense Typhoon Season**
    *   The region experienced a wave of powerful and unpredictable typhoons, exacerbated by the El Niño weather pattern.
    *   Impact: Dozens were killed, major airports closed, and crops and infrastructure suffered significant damage across the Philippines, Taiwan, China, and Japan.
    *   Outlook: Record warm Pacific Ocean temperatures suggest further severe storm activity is likely.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw33kgj7nkemo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw33kgj7nkemo))
