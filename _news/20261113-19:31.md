## Global Economic & Market Movers

*   **US Inflation & Fed Outlook:** Chicago Fed President Austan Goolsbee expressed concern over persistent inflation, indicating likely further interest rate hikes.
    *   Details: October's Producer Price Index (PPI) rose 0.3% (6.5% annually), the highest since November 2022. Consumer Price Index (CPI) also exceeded expectations.
    *   Impact: Markets are fully pricing in a quarter percentage point hike at the December Fed meeting, with inflation becoming more broad-based.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/chicago-feds-goolsbee-says-central-bank-has-definitely-got-work-to-do-on-inflation.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/chicago-feds-goolsbee-says-central-bank-has-definitely-got-work-to-do-on-inflation.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/ppi-inflation-report-october-2026.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/ppi-inflation-report-october-2026.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/treasury-yields-fed-rates.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/treasury-yields-fed-rates.html))
*   **Oil Market Surplus, Tight Diesel:** Russian crude oil production rebounded in October, contributing to a global oil market surplus.
    *   Details: Crude output rose by 210,000 b/d to 8.73 million b/d, pushing global supply (105.4 million b/d) above demand.
    *   Impact: Global oil markets tentatively rebalancing, but diesel supply remains constrained due to ongoing attacks on Russian infrastructure and Middle East disruptions.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/russia-oil-exports-iea-diesel-deal.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/russia-oil-exports-iea-diesel-deal.html))
*   **Wealthy Investors Shift to Alternatives:** The world's super-rich are increasing investments in alternative assets like hedge funds, private equity, and infrastructure.
    *   Context: Driven by concentrated equity markets, geopolitical uncertainty, and a desire for diversification and income.
    *   Impact: Highlights a strategic shift in wealth management, with infrastructure demand boosted by AI and energy security.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/rich-wealth-alternative-assets-.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/rich-wealth-alternative-assets-.html))

## Science, Tech, & Health (Major Developments)

*   **Global Microsoft Outage:** Microsoft 365 services, including Outlook, Teams, and Copilot, experienced a widespread global outage lasting nearly 24 hours.
    *   Cause: A technical issue during a network capacity upgrade, not a cyberattack.
    *   Impact: Affected schools, healthcare systems, businesses, courts, and governments across at least 74 countries. Email delivery was delayed.
    *   Resolution: Services are being gradually restored, though intermittent issues may persist.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwvg238zrnm5o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwvg238zrnm5o), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/microsoft-outage-outlook-aws-google.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/microsoft-outage-outlook-aws-google.html))
*   **Nvidia CEO Resigns Amid Scandal:** Jensen Huang, co-founder and CEO of Nvidia, resigned following revelations of extramarital affairs, including one with an alleged Chinese spy.
    *   Impact: The scandal led to a $1.6 trillion drop in Nvidia's market value and raised national security concerns for the US.
    *   Succession: CFO Colette Kress was named interim CEO; the board is seeking a permanent successor.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-jensen-huang-ceo-live-updates.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-jensen-huang-ceo-live-updates.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/colette-kress-nvidia-cfo-interim-ceo-candidate.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/colette-kress-nvidia-cfo-interim-ceo-candidate.html))

    *   Response: US authorities seized $1.2 billion in cryptocurrency from a previous Binance hack attributed to North Korea and disrupted FaceStealer infrastructure.
    *   Impact: Funds North Korea's illicit weapons programs; highlights evolving sophisticated cyber threats in the crypto and Web3 sectors.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqdj1ellpd70o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqdj1ellpd70o), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/us-seizes-binance-hack-crypto.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/us-seizes-binance-hack-crypto.html))
*   **OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Wallet:** OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet, enabling its "dots" AI agents to make purchases for users.
