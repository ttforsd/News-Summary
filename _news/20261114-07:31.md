## International Relations & Diplomacy

*   **US, China, Russia Leaders to Meet:** US President Trump, Russian President Putin, and Chinese President Xi Jinping are slated for a trilateral meeting next week at the APEC summit in Shenzhen, China.
    *   Detail: Discussions will focus on nuclear weapons reduction, with Iran also a potential topic.
    *   Context: This is a rare convergence of the three leaders, though no immediate agreements are expected.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckz74jzpy18do](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckz74jzpy18do), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/trump-nvidia-huang-china.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/trump-nvidia-huang-china.html))
*   **Russia Raises Ukraine Peace Demands:** President Putin insists on "eternal peace" for Ukraine, contingent on Russia's terms, including a new European security architecture.
    *   Detail: Russia revives its 2021 proposal to halt NATO enlargement and roll back military infrastructure in Eastern Europe.
    *   Impact: EU diplomats label Russia's demands a "trap," aiming to deflect from ceasefire efforts.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq39glwprknjo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq39glwprknjo))
*   **Germany Expands Spy Powers:** German parliament approved significant new powers for its foreign and domestic intelligence services.
    *   Detail: BND (foreign intelligence) can conduct covert operations abroad, including sabotage and disrupting disinformation networks. BfV (domestic intelligence) gains powers for protective action against IT systems and digital surveillance.
    *   Context: Aims to counter increasing "hybrid threats" and espionage from nations like Russia, China, and Iran.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6jre3z03w4go](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6jre3z03w4go))

## Major Regional Events

*   **US Air Travel Ground Stop:** The US Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) imposed a nationwide ground stop for over four hours, causing extensive flight delays and cancellations.
    *   Detail: The disruption, longest since 9/11, was attributed to an overloaded "incredibly old air traffic control system" due to analogue telecom equipment.
    *   Impact: Affected over 8,500 delayed and nearly 8,000 cancelled flights; international flights to US were also suspended. Transportation Secretary anticipates ongoing ripple effects for days.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqx2wyvyr3zzo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqx2wyvyr3zzo), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/faa-ground-stop.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/faa-ground-stop.html))
*   **Ecuador's Environmental-Economic Balancing Act:** Ecuador is pursuing green finance initiatives for conservation while simultaneously planning to expand oil exploration in the Amazon.
    *   Context: The "megadiverse" nation seeks to monetize biodiversity through bonds and debt-for-nature swaps, but faces opposition from indigenous groups over oil and mining.
    *   Outlook: Ecuador will host the UN biodiversity summit COP18 in 2028, spotlighting its complex environmental policies.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/14/ecuador-biodiversity-green-finance-oil.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/14/ecuador-biodiversity-green-finance-oil.html))

## Global Economic & Market Movers

*   **Nvidia CEO Scandal and Market Impact:** Former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned amidst revelations of extramarital affairs, including one with a suspected Chinese spy.
    *   Detail: US Treasury Secretary blamed the Commerce Department for delaying action against Megaspeed (the spy's firm), suspected of diverting Nvidia chips to China.
    *   Impact: Nvidia stock plunged, suffering its longest weekly losing streak since 2012, losing an estimated $1.7 trillion in market cap. Congress has initiated investigations.
    *   Market Shift: Investor interest is rotating toward rival chipmakers like AMD, Broadcom, and TSMC.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqvg2d1eg7zro](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqvg2d1eg7zro), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-jensen-huang-washington-congress.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-jensen-huang-washington-congress.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-stock-jensen-affair-ai.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-stock-jensen-affair-ai.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-jensen-huang-founder-ceo-transition.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-jensen-huang-founder-ceo-transition.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/alice-huang-jensen-huang-nvidia-huawei.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/alice-huang-jensen-huang-nvidia-huawei.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/why-singapore-keeps-turning-up-in-the-fight-over-nvidia-chips-and-china.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/why-singapore-keeps-turning-up-in-the-fight-over-nvidia-chips-and-china.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-turmoil-prompts-rotation-across-chip-stocks.html](https://www.cnbc.uk/news/articles/cqvg2d1eg7zro), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-without-jensen-huang-what-lies-ahead-for-asian-chipmakers-.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-without-jensen-huang-what-lies-ahead-for-asian-chipmakers-.html))
*   **US Inflationary Pressures:** US wholesale prices (PPI) rose 0.3% in October, bringing the annual rate to 6.5%, the highest in nearly four years.
    *   Detail: Chicago Fed President Austan Goolsbee expressed concern that inflation is spreading beyond initial shocks, suggesting the Fed "has definitely got work to do."
    *   Outlook: Markets are pricing in a probable quarter-point interest rate hike in December, with a slight chance of a half-point increase.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/chicago-feds-goolsbee-says-central-bank-has-definitely-got-work-to-do-on-inflation.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/chicago-feds-goolsbee-says-central-bank-has-definitely-got-work-to-do-on-inflation.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/ppi-inflation-report-october-2026.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/ppi-inflation-report-october-2026.html))
*   **Super-Rich Shift to Alternative Investments:** Wealthy investors are increasingly diversifying into hedge funds, private equity, and infrastructure assets.
    *   Context: This shift is driven by concentrated equity markets, geopolitical uncertainty, and concerns over persistent inflation.
    *   Impact: Infrastructure demand, boosted by AI and electrification, is a significant beneficiary of this trend.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/rich-wealth-alternative-assets-.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/rich-wealth-alternative-assets-.html))
*   **Canada Threatens Stellantis over Production Shift:** Canada is threatening legal action against automaker Stellantis for alleged breaches of a federal funding agreement.
    *   Detail: Stellantis plans to sell its Brampton plant and move Jeep Compass production to the US, influenced by Trump's trade war tariffs.
    *   Impact: Canada seeks hundreds of millions in compensation, highlighting ongoing US-Canada trade tensions affecting manufacturing.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/canada-threatens-stellantis-as-trump-trade-war-reshapes-jeep-production-plans.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/canada-stellantis-auto-trump-tariffs-jeep.html))

## Science, Tech, & Health (Major Developments)

*   **Microsoft Global Outage Restored:** Microsoft has fully restored its services after a day-long worldwide outage affected Outlook, Teams, and Copilot.
    *   Detail: The disruption, which hit over 70 countries and 430 million users, was caused by a "network capacity upgrade" issue, not a cyberattack.
    *   Impact: The incident underscored global reliance on major tech providers for critical business and government functions.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6rrg51rkz09o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6rrg51rkz09o), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/microsoft-outage-outlook-aws-google.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/microsoft-outage-outlook-aws-google.html))
*   **SpaceX Starship Booster Fails Recovery:** SpaceX's 15th Starship test flight experienced a setback when the Super Heavy booster failed its "double-catch" attempt.
    *   Detail: The booster was intentionally diverted to splash down in the Gulf of Mexico, where it exploded. The upper stage is continuing to orbit, deploying Starlink satellites.
    *   Context: The full reusability goal is crucial for reducing space travel costs and supporting NASA's Artemis III lunar mission.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckregdgnl0xpo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckregdgnl0xpo), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/spacex-launches-starship-in-key-test-flight-of-massive-rocket.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/spacex-launches-starship-in-key-test-flight-of-massive-rocket.html))
*   **AI Risks Top Financial System Threats:** A Federal Reserve survey found that artificial intelligence risks are now the primary concern for financial system stability.
    *   Detail: 87% of respondents cited AI as a "salient risk," due to concerns over elevated equity valuations, cybersecurity (including state actors and AI models themselves), and complex AI-related financing.
    *   Impact: This highlights growing regulatory vigilance over AI's potential systemic financial dangers.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/ai-risks-financial-system-federal-reserve.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/ai-risks-financial-system-federal-reserve.html))
*   **OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Wallet:** OpenAI has introduced ChatGPT Wallet, enabling its "dots" AI agents to make purchases on behalf of users.
    *   Detail: The feature, built in partnership with American Express, Mastercard, and Visa, is initially for US Pro subscribers (18+).
    *   Impact: Aims to kickstart a new revenue stream for OpenAI and position itself as a serious platform for agentic commerce.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/openai-chatgpt-wallet-dot-purchases.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/openai-chatgpt-wallet-dot-purchases.html))
*   **AI Reshaping Consulting Industry:** Artificial intelligence is challenging traditional business models in the consulting sector.
    *   Detail: AI automates entry-level tasks like research and data analysis, pressuring time-based pricing models and affecting graduate career paths.
    *   Impact: Consulting firms are redefining roles and training, while smaller AI-native consultancies are leveraging their agility.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/ai-consulting-billable-hours-graduate-hiring.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/ai-consulting-billable-hours-graduate-hiring.html))
*   **US Seizes $1.2 Billion in Stolen Crypto:** US authorities seized approximately $1.2 billion in cryptocurrency stolen from Binance in an October 29 hack.
    *   Detail: The Department of Justice attributed the hack to North Korean military actors ("TraderTraitor"). Over $2.7 billion has been frozen in total.
    *   Context: This is part of a broader crackdown on North Korean cybercrime funding its regime and illegal weapons programs.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/us-seizes-binance-hack-crypto.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/us-seizes-binance-hack-crypto.html))
*   **Aide to Ukrainian Ex-PM Wounded in Moscow Car Bombing:** Sergei Zavorotny, a former advisor to ex-PM Mykola Azarov, was wounded in a car bomb attack in Moscow.
    *   Context: This is the latest in a series of attacks on figures linked to Russia's invasion of Ukraine, following a Russian colonel's death in St Petersburg.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5gm3gnwl42eo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5gm3gnwl42eo))
