## Global Economic & Market Movers

*   **U.S. Wholesale Prices Rise Significantly, Federal Reserve Concerned About Inflation**
    *   The Producer Price Index (PPI) rose 0.3% in October, with an annual rate reaching 6.5%, the highest since November 2022.
    *   Chicago Fed President Austan Goolsbee expressed concern that inflation is spreading beyond typical factors and indicated the Fed "has work to do."
    *   Markets are pricing in a likely quarter-percentage-point interest rate hike at the Fed's December meeting.
    *   Sources: ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/chicago-feds-goolsbee-says-central-bank-has-definitely-got-work-to-do-on-inflation.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/chicago-feds-goolsbee-says-central-bank-has-definitely-got-work-to-do-on-inflation.html)), ([https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/ppi-inflation-report-october-2026.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/ppi-inflation-report-october-2026.html))

*   **Ecuador Seeks to Monetize Biodiversity Amidst Oil Drilling Plans**

    *   This dual strategy draws opposition from Indigenous and environmental groups.
    *   Ecuador aims to host the UN biodiversity COP18 in 2028 and issue its first sovereign green bond in 2027.

    *   This shift is driven by market volatility, geopolitical uncertainty, and concerns over concentrated equity markets and inflation.

*   **Russian Crude Production Rebounds Following U.S. Diesel Deal**
    *   Russia's crude oil production rose by 210,000 barrels a day in October to 8.73 million barrels a day, according to the IEA.
    *   This rebound, partly due to a U.S. deal to supply Russian diesel to global markets, has contributed to global oil markets returning to a surplus for the first time since the Iran war began.
    *   Diesel supply remains constrained due to ongoing attacks on Russian infrastructure and Middle East disruptions.
