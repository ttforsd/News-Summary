## Global Economic & Market Movers

*   **Nvidia Stock Decline Amid CEO Fallout:**
    *   Nvidia's stock fell, closing below $4 trillion market cap for the first time since July 2025.
    *   This marks its sixth straight weekly decline following CEO Jensen Huang's resignation due to alleged affairs.
    *   (Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-closes-below-4t-market-cap-for-first-time-since-july-2025-in-huang-fallout.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-closes-below-4t-market-cap-for-first-time-since-july-2025-in-huang-fallout.html))
*   **US Flight Ground Stop Due to Infrastructure Issue:**
    *   A nationwide US ground stop was caused by a malfunctioning router during a copper-to-fiber wire upgrade.
    *   Thousands of flights were cancelled/delayed; Transportation Secretary calls for significant infrastructure funding.
    *   (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6wy9lynq4qno](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6wy9lynq4qno), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/14/dot-sec-duffy-ties-router-in-southern-arkansas-to-issues-that-led-to-nationwide-ground-stop.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/14/dot-sec-duffy-ties-router-in-southern-arkansas-to-issues-that-led-to-nationwide-ground-stop.html))
*   **Fed Warns AI Risks Threaten Financial System:**
    *   A Federal Reserve survey identified AI risks (equity valuations, cybersecurity, complex financing) as the primary threat to financial stability.
    *   87% of respondents cited AI, a substantial increase from previous surveys.
    *   (Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/ai-risks-financial-system-federal-reserve.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/ai-risks-financial-system-federal-reserve.html))
*   **Chicago Fed Expresses Inflation Concerns:**
    *   Chicago Fed President Austan Goolsbee noted inflation is "spreading" and the central bank needs to rebuild credibility.
    *   The Producer Price Index increased, indicating persistent inflationary pressures, partly from AI data center buildout.
    *   (Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/chicago-feds-goolsbee-says-central-bank-has-definitely-got-work-to-do-on-inflation.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/chicago-feds-goolsbee-says-central-bank-has-definitely-got-work-to-do-on-inflation.html))
*   **Italy Fines Meta €805M for Data Breach:**

    *   Meta allegedly failed to inform 20 million users about the Facebook app's photo suggestion feature.
    *   (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwn83gxemk35o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwn83gxemk35o))
*   **US Seizes $1.2B in Stolen Crypto from Binance:**

## Science, Tech, & Health (Major Developments)

*   **SpaceX Starship Achieves First Upper Stage Catch:**
    *   SpaceX successfully caught the upper stage of its Starship rocket using mechanical arms ("chopsticks") upon return to Earth.
