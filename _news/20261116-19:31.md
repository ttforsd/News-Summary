## Global Economic & Market Movers

*   **IMF Warns on AI Over-reliance:** IMF Managing Director Kristalina Georgieva states the global economy is "standing on one leg" – artificial intelligence.
    *   Warns that overdependence on AI could risk financial stability if the sector wobbles.
    *   Circular financing among AI companies is over $1 trillion, two-thirds funded by debt.
    *   (Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/imf-georgieva-warning-ai-circular-financing.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/imf-georgieva-warning-ai-circular-financing.html))
*   **Japan's Economy Contracts:** Japan's economy unexpectedly shrank at an annualized 0.1% in Q3, missing forecasts.
    *   Elevated energy costs weighed on consumption and net exports.
    *   This complicates the Bank of Japan's policy normalization path.
    *   (Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/japan-q3-gdp-energy-oil-prices.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/japan-q3-gdp-energy-oil-prices.html))
*   **US Crude Oil Prices Decline:** Oil prices fell as Middle East exports recover and US-Iran tensions remain at a stalemate.
    *   IEA expects global supply to exceed demand in Q4 2026, reversing earlier deficit forecasts.
    *   (Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/us-crude-oil-declines-to-87-a-barrel-on-recovering-middle-east-exports.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/us-crude-oil-declines-to-87-a-barrel-on-recovering-middle-east-exports.html))
*   **AI Reshaping Banking and Consulting Jobs:** Banks are automating routine work, leading to job cuts in support functions (e.g., Barclays).
    *   Demand shifts to AI, software engineering, and data skills in finance.
    *   Consulting firms are reconsidering traditional time-based billing and graduate pipelines as AI automates entry-level tasks.
    *   (Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/how-ai-is-changing-banking-jobs-hiring-and-revenue.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/ai-is-reshaping-banking-jobs-but-its-ability-to-drive-profits-remains-unclear-.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/ai-consulting-billable-hours-graduate-hiring.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/ai-consulting-billable-hours-and-its-graduate-pipeline.html))
