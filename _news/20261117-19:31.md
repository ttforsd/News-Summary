## Major Regional Events

*   **Ukraine War: Key Bridges Targeted in Crimea and Kyiv**
    *   Ukraine inflicted severe damage on the Kerch Bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea, causing a large section to collapse.
    *   President Zelensky hailed the attack as an "unprecedented operation" weeks in the making.
    *   Russia retaliated with missile strikes on Kyiv's Northern and Southern bridges, disrupting transport, heating, and water.
    *   (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw772jk4rvmxo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw772jk4rvmxo), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/crz9dgz28zwdo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/crz9dgz28zwdo), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq7825234lgmo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq7825234lgmo), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/kerch-bridge-ukraine-russia-putin-kyiv.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/kerch-bridge-ukraine-russia-putin-kyiv.html))

*   **Violence in the Occupied West Bank**
    *   A 72-year-old Palestinian woman was shot and killed by Israeli settlers in al-Duyuk al-Tahta village, near Jericho.
    *   Conflicting accounts emerged, with Palestinians reporting settler attacks and the Israeli army citing a car-ramming.
    *   (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqrlg377638yo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqrlg377638yo))

    *   Former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned amid revelations of extramarital affairs, including with an alleged Chinese spy.
    *   Huang expressed willingness to return, but Nvidia's board is not considering it; a new committee oversees national security policies.

## Science, Tech, & Health (Major Developments)

*   **US Loses Measles Elimination Status**
    *   The Pan American Health Organization (PAHO) revoked the US's measles-free status after 26 years.
    *   Uninterrupted transmission, record cases (5,400) and 10 deaths this year are linked to declining vaccination rates below herd immunity.
    *   (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c977p71jx0xxo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c977p71jx0xxo))

*   **Spain Considers Tighter Data Center Regulations**
    *   Spain's government is moving to approve new rules requiring large data centers to meet stringent renewable energy conditions.
    *   The proposed policy addresses growing concerns over energy and water consumption, potentially affecting billions in tech investment.
