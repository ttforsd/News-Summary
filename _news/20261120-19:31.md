## Executive Summary

*   **Ukraine War Escalates, Poland Hit:** Russia launched massive missile and drone strikes on Ukraine, killing civilians, days after Ukraine hit the Crimean Bridge. A Russian missile also violated Polish airspace during NATO exercises, escalating regional tensions.
*   **APEC Summit and Geopolitical Shifts:** Trump, Xi, and Putin held historic trilateral talks, issuing a "joint declaration of peace." Putin gained diplomatic standing, but key US-China issues remain unresolved. Trump announced a plan to ban US companies from building data centers abroad and impose taxes on those that do.
*   **AI Market Volatility & Tech Shake-Up:** Anthropic's IPO saw shares drop after a strong opening, amidst a broader tech sell-off pushing major US indices into correction territory. Bitcoin also saw significant drops, challenging its "digital gold" narrative.
*   **Pancreatic Cancer Breakthrough:** Early trials for a new daily pill, daraxonrasib, showed remarkable success, shrinking or eliminating tumors in a significant number of advanced pancreatic cancer patients.
*   **US Cyber Leadership Change:** The White House removed its National Cyber Director, Sean Cairncross, following a series of widespread tech outages impacting critical US infrastructure.

## Science, Tech, & Health (Major Developments)

*   **Breakthrough in Pancreatic Cancer Treatment:**
    *   A new daily pill, daraxonrasib, showed promising results in early clinical trials for advanced pancreatic cancer.
    *   Tumors stopped growing in 90% of patients, and became undetectable in 8% (1 in 12) of participants.
    *   This is hailed as the "biggest leap forward in decades" for this highly lethal cancer, also showing potential for other cancers with RAS mutations.
    *   Sources: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqrmgjnzz9l0o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqrmgjnzz9l0o)
*   **US Cyber Director Replaced Amid Widespread Tech Outages:**
    *   White House National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross was removed by President Trump, who stated Cairncross was "not good enough."
    *   Kirsten Davies, Pentagon CIO, is appointed as acting director.
    *   This follows a series of tech outages affecting banking, air traffic control, and NSA systems, though no coordinated cyberattack is indicated.
    *   Sources: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwz0k3nn5p5mo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwz0k3nn5p5mo), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/white-house-replaces-cyber-director-sean-cairncross-amid-widespread-tech-outages.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/white-house-replaces-cyber-director-sean-cairncross-amid-widespread-tech-outages.html)
