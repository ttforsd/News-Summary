## Major regional Events

*   **France Faces Recession Risk and Social Unrest:**
    *   France's private sector activity reached a six-month low in November, with job cuts for the seventh consecutive month.
    *   Ongoing student-led protests against rising costs, poor public services, and police brutality have turned violent.
    *   Impact: Economic uncertainty is exacerbated by upcoming 2027 presidential elections; some analysts warn of potential recession.
    *   (Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/france-recession-risk-pmi.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/france-recession-risk-pmi.html), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw074km48myeo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw074km48myeo))

*   **Gaza Declared "Toxic Wasteland":**
    *   An Oxfam report states over 99% of Gaza shows environmental contamination from Israeli military attacks.
    *   Heavy metals, explosives, asbestos, and untreated sewage threaten human health for decades.
    *   Impact: Increased respiratory diseases, cancers, and birth defects are projected; Israeli embassy criticizes report methodology.
    *   (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8r4gzzxvxl3o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8r4gzzxvxl3o))

*   **Bangladesh Ex-PM Plans Return Amid Unrest:**

    *   She was sentenced to death in absentia for crimes against humanity, which she denies.
    *   Impact: Announcement coincides with renewed protests by her party; Bangladeshi government vows to arrest her.
    *   (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c60rk1yj07xeo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c60rk1yj07xeo))

*   **Iran Flogs Singer for Hijab Violation:**
    *   Iranian authorities publicly flogged singer Parastoo Ahmadi and four band members for performing without a mandatory hijab.

*   **South Africa's Femicide Crisis:**

    *   Local communities are organizing night patrols and curfews due to perceived police inaction.
    *   Impact: Calls for greater police resources and effectiveness in tackling femicide; government promises increased budget and policing.

## Global Economic & Market Movers

*   **Nvidia CEO Reinstated Amid Political Pressure:**
    *   Jensen Huang returned as Nvidia CEO, 11 days after resigning due to alleged affairs, including with a suspected Chinese spy.
    *   President Trump claimed to have "made the deal" to convince the board, citing national security assurances.
    *   Impact: Nvidia stock surged 31% adding nearly $1 trillion in market value; bipartisan Congressional backlash demands investigations into US-China tech policy and Huang's conduct.
    *   (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq5y1k8ez407o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq5y1k8ez407o), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/trump-nvidia-jensen-huang.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/trump-nvidia-jensen-huang.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-jensen-huang-congress-trump.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-jensen-huang-congress-trump.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-stock-jensen-huang-ai-trade.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-stock-jensen-huang-ai-trade.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/nvidia-jensen-huang-governance.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/nvidia-jensen-huang-governance.html))

*   **US Blacklists Megaspeed over Export Concerns:**
    *   The Department of Commerce added Singapore-based AI firm Megaspeed to a trade blacklist.
    *   Action taken due to "significant risk" of diverting advanced computing products, including Nvidia chips, to unauthorized users in China.
    *   Impact: Intensifies US-China tech rivalry; Singapore PM states country does not want to be a conduit for export control circumvention.
    *   (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cv98xpg5dg5zo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cv98xpg5dg5zo), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/us-adds-megaspeed-to-trade-blacklist-over-export-control-concerns-.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/us-adds-megaspeed-to-trade-blacklist-over-export-control-concerns-.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-megaspeed-chipmaker-us.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-megaspeed-chipmaker-us.html))

*   **Asian Markets Rally on Nvidia News:**
    *   Key Nvidia partners and suppliers across Asia saw shares surge, with Taiwan's TSMC and Foxconn nearing daily price limits.
    *   South Korea's Samsung and SK Hynix also rose significantly; Japan's Softbank Group climbed over 8%.
    *   Impact: Investor confidence in the AI sector partially restored, but long-term sustainability hinges on demand and overcoming memory chip shortages.
    *   (Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/nvidia-jensen-huang-asia-chip-stocks.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/nvidia-jensen-huang-asia-chip-stocks.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/how-asian-countries-are-looking-to-prop-up-markets-as-ai-downturn-sinks-stocks.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/how-asian-countries-are-looking-to-prop-up-markets-as-ai-downturn-sinks-stocks.html))

*   **Global Climate Talks Collapse (COP31):**
    *   The UN's COP31 climate summit in Turkey ended without a consensus on a central cover decision.
    *   Key failure point was the exclusion of language on phasing out fossil fuels, leading to rejection by the EU and others.
    *   Impact: Raises questions about multilateralism's effectiveness in climate action; some progress on electrification pledge, but finance gaps remain for developing nations.
    *   (Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm75237wlp3lo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm75237wlp3lo), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/cop31-climate-summit-fossil-fuels.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/cop31-climate-summit-fossil-fuels.html))

*   **Tokenized Stocks Face Pushback:**
    *   OKXICE, a new platform for tokenized securities, debuted with 90% of targeted companies objecting to having their shares listed.
    *   Concerns include readiness for 24/7 trading and impact on investor relations.
    *   Impact: Highlights challenges and varied approaches in integrating traditional finance with blockchain technology; SEC's Innovation Exemption allows temporary trading.
    *   (Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/tokenized-stocks-face-pushback-as-nyse-nasdaq-pivot-for-a-new-market-.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/tokenized-stocks-face-pushback-as-nyse-nasdaq-pivot-for-a-new-market-.html))
