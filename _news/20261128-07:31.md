## Executive summary

## International Relations & Diplomacy

*   **Ukraine-US Tensions over Aid and Peace Talks**
    *   President Zelensky criticized US delays in anti-ballistic missile deliveries, impacting Ukrainian lives.
    *   He also voiced frustration over former President Trump's "strange" friendship with Vladimir Putin.
    *   Trump and NATO chief Rutte issued a joint statement, urging Russia to avoid war escalation and seek peace.
    *   Ukrainian forces continue long-range strikes, including a Russian warship, while Kyiv braces for winter attacks.
    *   Sources: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c820q9wgrpyyo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c820q9wgrpyyo), [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3pw4yrr8kz9o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3pw4yrr8kz9o), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/trump-nato-rutte-russia-ukraine-war.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/trump-nato-rutte-russia-ukraine-war.html)
*   **US Tariff Policies and Trade Relations**
    *   The US proposed new tariffs on 14 trading partners, including China, Vietnam, and India.
    *   These tariffs aim to curb excess industrial capacity and encourage reciprocal trade agreements.
    *   India could receive a preferential 17% tariff rate if it finalizes a trade deal with the US.
    *   Sources: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/us-tariffs-hike-trade-agreement.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/us-tariffs-hike-trade-agreement.html), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/trump-us-tariff-india-trade.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/trump-us-tariff-india-trade.html)
*   **Iran-Kuwait Tensions**
    *   Two Italian citizens were killed in Kuwait following alleged Iranian drone attacks.
    *   Kuwaiti forces attributed the deaths to falling missile debris; Iran denied involvement and called the incident "suspicious."
    *   Former President Trump asserted the Iran conflict would end "whenever I want it," as Israel reportedly sought US backing for joint strikes.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-italian-killed-trump.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-italian-killed-trump.html)

## Major Regional Events

*   **Spanish General Election Campaign Closes**
    *   Spain's main party leaders held final rallies ahead of Sunday's snap election.
    *   Polls indicate a narrow lead for the conservative People's Party (PP), potentially forming a coalition with the far-right Vox.
    *   A right-wing government could shift Spain's foreign policy closer to the US stance.
    *   Housing and cost of living remain key voter concerns amidst national protests.
    *   Sources: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8wy5p4gdnwvo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8wy5p4gdnwvo), [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/spain-election-trump-sanchez.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/spain-election-trump-sanchez.html)
*   **EU Leaders Fail to Agree on Budget**
    *   European Union leaders concluded a 12-hour summit without reaching a consensus on the bloc's €1.58 trillion budget for 2028-2034.
    *   Germany and other net contributors pushed for deeper cuts, while most members opposed further reductions.
    *   Talks are slated to resume in mid-December, intensified by upcoming national elections, including France's presidential race.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/eu-budget-2028-2034-deadlock.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/eu-budget-2028-2034-deadlock.html)
*   **Peruvian Military Helicopter Crash**
    *   Eight military personnel died in a helicopter crash in Peru's VRAEM region, known for drug trafficking.
    *   The sole survivor was the pilot; the aircraft was on a mission to evacuate an army patrol.
    *   Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwp9d85mlp88o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwp9d85mlp88o)
*   **Turkey's Crackdown on Opposition Media**
    *   The London-based owner of Halk TV, a prominent pro-opposition channel, claimed Turkish authorities have "destroyed" his network.
    *   Nine individuals connected to Halk TV were arrested on charges of money laundering, bribery, and spreading misinformation.
    *   This is part of President Erdoğan's broader clampdown on dissent following his 2023 election victory.
    *   Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq36gk151xkdo](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq36gk151xkdo)
*   **Quebec's Religious Symbols Ban Upheld**
    *   Canada's Supreme Court largely affirmed Quebec's Bill 21, which prohibits public sector workers from wearing religious symbols.
    *   The ruling, however, states the law infringes on democratic rights by banning elected officials from wearing face coverings.
    *   Critics argue the law disproportionately targets Muslim women and deepens social divides.
    *   Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq3dgpy9lem3o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq3dgpy9lem3o)
*   **Marital Rape Laws in South Asia**
    *   A new study highlights that millions of women in eight South Asian countries lack legal protection against marital rape.
    *   India, Afghanistan, Sri Lanka, and Bangladesh have not fully criminalized it, retaining colonial-era exemptions.
    *   Nepal and Bhutan have criminalized marital rape but impose lesser penalties than other rape offenses.
    *   Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw074p95ye5do](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw074p95ye5do)
*   **China's Growing Influence and Suppressed Voices**
    *   Uyghur and Tibetan activists report that China's economic power increasingly stifles international attention on human rights abuses.
    *   Beijing allegedly uses incentives like family reunions to gather intelligence from exiles and promote pro-party narratives.
    *   Source: [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cme3d4y094x7o](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cme3d4y094x7o)

## Global Economic & Market Movers

*   **Saudi Arabia Recalibrates Vision 2030**
    *   Economy Minister Alibrahim announced a shift in Vision 2030 spending from "at any cost" to "at the right cost."
    *   This adjustment follows a 3.8% real GDP contraction in Q3 and persistent Houthi attacks.
    *   The Kingdom aims for more precise investment and increased private sector participation.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/28/saudi-arabia-vision-2030-spending.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/28/saudi-arabia-vision-2030-spending.html)
*   **Africa's Largest Refinery IPO**
    *   Nigeria's Dangote Petroleum Refinery is preparing for Africa's biggest IPO, with an estimated valuation of $47 billion.
    *   The offering was heavily oversubscribed globally and is set to list in Lagos on December 1st.
    *   The refinery has emerged as a crucial jet fuel supplier, enhancing supply to Atlantic Basin markets.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/28/dangote-refinery-oil-ipo-nigeria-africa-energy.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/28/dangote-refinery-oil-ipo-nigeria-africa-energy.html)
*   **European Defense Industry Reins in Growth**
    *   German arms maker Rheinmetall lowered its 2030 sales guidance due to project delays and a significant lost contract.
    *   The company is adopting a "more cautious" approach amidst Europe's rearmament efforts.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/rheinmetall-stock-cmd-guidance-sales.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/rheinmetall-stock-cmd-guidance-sales.html)
*   **Aluminum Supply Crunch Deepens**
    *   Available aluminum stocks in London Metal Exchange (LME) warehouses have halved since the Iran war began.
    *   The LME intervened with new rules to ease severe market backwardation, which saw immediate price spread narrowing.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/lme-aluminum-squeeze-supply.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/lme-aluminum-squeeze-supply.html)
*   **Norse Atlantic Files for Bankruptcy**
    *   Budget airline Norse Atlantic suspended all flights and filed for bankruptcy due to insufficient funding.
    *   Soaring jet fuel costs since the Iran war significantly strained the company's operations.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/norse-atlantic-bankruptcy-fuel-costs.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/norse-atlantic-bankruptcy-fuel-costs.html)
*   **India's Auto Sector Records Stellar Sales**
    *   India's auto industry is projected to achieve record sales, exceeding 30 million vehicles by March 2027.
    *   Robust domestic demand, enhanced road connectivity, and accessible financing fuel this growth.
    *   Major automakers are expanding production capacity to meet the rising consumer demand.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/india-auto-slaes-economy-suzuki-hyundai-toyota.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/india-auto-slaes-economy-suzuki-hyundai-toyota.html)
*   **Sovereign Wealth Funds Shift Investment Strategy**
    *   Sovereign wealth funds are increasingly adopting private equity tactics, making direct investments and taking control stakes.
    *   Their combined $16.5 trillion in assets grants significant bargaining power and potential fee savings.
    *   This strategy exposes SWFs to higher operational and concentration risks inherent in direct investing.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/swf-private-equity-risks.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/swf-private-equity-risks.html)
*   **Spain's Pablo Hernández de Cos to Lead ECB**
    *   Pablo Hernández de Cos, former Bank of Spain Governor, is set to become the next European Central Bank president.
    *   His appointment has broad euro zone support; he is known for pragmatic, data-driven monetary policy.
    *   De Cos will navigate challenges including persistent inflation, economic divergence, and political uncertainty.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/pablo-hernandez-de-cos-ecb-president.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/pablo-hernandez-de-cos-ecb-president.html)

## Science, Tech, & Health (Major Developments)

*   **Semiconductor Exports Exceed Oil/Gas Value**
    *   Semiconductor exports reached $1.61 trillion in Q1 2026, surpassing oil and gas in global export value for the first time.
    *   The artificial intelligence boom is driving unprecedented demand for chips and data center infrastructure globally.
    *   This shift carries significant macroeconomic implications, potentially altering global financial flows.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/semiconductor-chips-are-the-new-oil-heres-why.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/semiconductor-chips-are-the-new-oil-heres-why.html)
*   **US Intensifies AI Chip Controls on China**
    *   Nvidia chip sellers are reportedly requiring buyers to exclude Chinese renters from computing capacity to purchase GPUs.
    *   A new Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) rule aims to close "loopholes" allowing Chinese companies remote access to advanced US computing.
    *   This measure further tightens US export controls and exacerbates US-China tech rivalry.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/neoclouds-nvidia-china-ai.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/neoclouds-nvidia-china-ai.html)
*   **AI Market Volatility and NVIDIA Leadership**
    *   Nvidia stock experienced significant volatility following CEO Jensen Huang's temporary resignation and rapid return.
    *   While Huang's return addressed leadership uncertainty, investors still weigh AI investment returns and broader market factors.
    *   Other AI-related stocks have also shown volatility amid questions about the AI boom's sustainability.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/the-tech-download-jensen-huang-ai-market.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/the-tech-download-jensen-huang-ai-market.html)
*   **SAP Plans Mass Job Cuts for AI Pivot**
    *   German software giant SAP is reportedly planning to cut at least 10,000 jobs as part of a restructuring focused on artificial intelligence.
    *   This move aims to reshape the company's workforce and operations for the evolving AI landscape.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/sap-job-cuts-ai-restructuring.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/27/sap-job-cuts-ai-restructuring.html)
*   **Europe Boosts Satellite Production to Rival Starlink**
    *   Europe's largest satellite factory opened in Belgium, aiming to produce up to 500 satellites annually.
    *   Aerospacelab secured a €2.4 billion contract for IRIS², the EU's secure connectivity infrastructure.
    *   This initiative seeks to enhance European space sovereignty and reduce reliance on US space systems.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/starlink-satellites-aerospacelab-factory-belgium.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/starlink-satellites-aerospacelab-factory-belgium.html)
*   **Latin America's Resources for AI Development**
    *   Latin America's copper, energy, and mineral resources are increasingly valuable for global AI build-out and electrification.
    *   Countries like Chile, Argentina, and Brazil offer significant potential in copper supply, oil/gas, renewable power, and rare earths.
    *   Challenges remain in developing adequate infrastructure, securing financing, and ensuring policy stability.
    *   Source: [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/latin-america-copper-energy-ai-investment.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/latin-america-copper-energy-ai-investment.html)
*   **Chinese EV Firm Predicts Household Robots in 1-2 Years**
    *   Xpeng, a Chinese electric vehicle company, anticipates humanoid robots will perform household chores within one to two years.
    *   The company plans to deploy its next-generation humanoid, Iron, for commercial training starting Q1 next year.
